## Four questions to ask any memory app

A memory app is only as private as where it stores your history and which model reads it. Ask four questions:

1. **Capture.** What does it save, and when?
2. **Storage.** Where does the history live?
3. **Inference.** Which model reads it, and where does that model run?
4. **Actions.** Can it do work for you, and who approves it?

## Where the two really differ

**Inference has a cloud path in Screenpipe.** Its [data flow page](https://docs.screenpipe.com/privacy-data-flow) is clear. Chat with local Ollama is "Local only." Chat with a cloud AI means "selected context is sent to that AI provider." That is fair and documented. It also means your privacy depends on the model you pick. In OGAD, the models that read your memory are ones you download and run on your computer.

**The licence is different.** Screenpipe's [README](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe) permits personal, non-commercial use of the source. Commercial use requires a licence. OGAD is AGPL-3.0, free to use at work, with no account.

**Capture is heavier in Screenpipe.** Its pricing page lists about 30 GB of storage a month and 600 MB to 4 GB of RAM, depending on settings. OGAD saves sampled moments, not a continuous record. On Windows and Linux, Overnight mode runs the analysis from 03:00 to 08:00, so your computer stays fast by day.

**Screenpipe is a memory layer. OGAD also does the work.** Screenpipe gives other agents your context through its API and MCP. OGAD answers questions with sources, builds your **Day** view and **People**, and drafts the follow-ups for you to approve.

## FAQ

**Is Screenpipe local?**
Yes. Screenpipe stores recordings on your computer by default. Chat with a local Ollama model stays local. If you pick a cloud AI provider, Screenpipe's docs say selected context is sent to that provider.

**Is Screenpipe open source?**
Its source code is public on GitHub under the Screenpipe Commercial License. Personal, non-commercial use is permitted. Commercial use needs a licence. Off Grid AI Desktop is open source under AGPL-3.0.

**How much does Screenpipe cost compared with Off Grid AI?**
Screenpipe Basic is $21 a month or $250 a year for full history. On 30 October 2026 Off Grid AI Pro is $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price, or $7.99 a month.

**Does Off Grid AI record video of my screen?**
No. After you choose Resume capture, it saves sampled screen moments on your disk. A local vision model writes a note for each one. You can pause capture, exclude apps and delete moments.

**Can Off Grid AI act on what it remembers?**
Yes. It drafts replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, and sends them only after you approve.

## Why few AI apps sell lifetime deals

Cloud AI costs the company every time you press enter, so the bill repeats. A lifetime deal only holds up when the model runs on your own hardware. That is why every app below runs models locally, or sells a device.

## How we ranked

1. **What one payment unlocks.** One feature, or a whole working day.
2. **What still costs money after you pay.** Minute caps, credits or a plan.
3. **Devices per payment,** and which operating systems.
4. **The one-time price.**

## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

**What it is.** Pro unlocks the paid half of Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) and the phone app (OGAM). Memory of your screens, meetings, mail and documents. Meetings recorded without a bot, transcribed with local Whisper and summarised by a local model. Actions in Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion that wait for your yes. God, a chief of staff with a morning brief and meeting prep. Reflect, Vault, Clipboard, Sync device to device, and dictation into any app.

**Best for.** Anyone who would otherwise pay for a notetaker, a dictation app and an AI assistant every month.

**Price.** $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. Up to 5 devices. No account. The base apps are free and open source.

**The catch.** Your computer does the AI work, so it needs to fit a local model; the app shows which fit. God's routines need the computer awake. Dictation into other apps is desktop only. The lifetime price is a limited-time offer.

## 2. Superwhisper Pro

**What it is.** A polished dictation app. The free plan has unlimited local Whisper dictation; Pro adds cloud models such as GPT-5 and Claude Haiku 4.5.

**Best for.** Dictation on every device, phones included. Its FAQ says a Pro license works on as many of your devices as you like.

**Price.** $249.99 lifetime, or $8.49 a month or $84.99 a year.

**The catch.** Three times the price of Off Grid AI Pro, no Linux, and Meetings is Mac only. See [Off Grid AI vs Superwhisper](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-superwhisper-2026-local-dictation-plus-meetings-memory-and-linux/).

## 3. MacWhisper Pro

**What it is.** Local transcription for the Mac, with system-wide dictation and bot-free meeting recording.

**Best for.** Mac users who want a transcript tool.

**Price.** €64 once, with lifetime updates. There is a free version.

**The catch.** Mac only, one license, no memory or follow-ups. See [Off Grid AI vs MacWhisper](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-macwhisper-2026-local-transcription-on-every-computer-not-just-mac/).

## 4. VoiceInk

**What it is.** An open-source dictation app that "processes all voice transcription locally" on Apple silicon Macs, with an iOS version.

**Best for.** The lowest one-time price for Mac dictation.

**Price.** Solo $25 for 1 Mac, Personal $39 for 2, Extended $49 for 3.

**The catch.** Apple silicon and macOS 14.4 or later only. Dictation is all it does.

## 5. Msty Studio Aurum Lifetime

**What it is.** An AI workspace that runs local models through Ollama, MLX and llama.cpp, and many cloud providers, with Knowledge Stacks for your files.

**Best for.** Side-by-side model comparison and Intel Macs. Msty lists SSO and an audit log for its Enterprise and Teams plans.

**Price.** $349 per user once, or $149 per user a year.

**The catch.** It costs over four times Off Grid AI Pro, per user. See [Off Grid AI vs Msty](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-msty-2026-a-private-ai-workspace-for-79-once-not-349/).

## 6. Private LLM

**What it is.** An on-device chat app for Apple devices.

**Best for.** The cheapest one-time buy for private chat on an iPhone or Mac.

**Price.** $4.99 once on the US App Store. Prices differ by region.

**The catch.** Apple only, and no images or file answers listed. Off Grid AI's free app already does local chat on every platform. See [Off Grid AI vs Private LLM](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-private-llm-2026-free-open-source-and-on-android-and-windows-too/).

## 7. Plaud NotePin

**What it is.** A $159 wearable recorder with transcripts from Plaud's AI service.

**Best for.** Meetings away from a laptop.

**Price.** $159 for the device, with a free Starter plan of 300 minutes a month.

**The catch.** Beyond 300 minutes you need Pro at $99.99 a year or Unlimited at $239.99 a year, so it isn't truly one-time if you record a lot. See [Off Grid AI vs Plaud](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-plaud-2026-no-recorder-to-buy-no-minutes-to-count/).

## Two ways to run Claw, both on credits

Genspark's [help center](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/genspark-claw) describes two modes.

- **Cloud computer.** A dedicated machine that Genspark runs for you, 24/7. It needs a separate Cloud Computer subscription, in Lite, Standard or Powerful tiers. Your app logins are saved in a browser on that cloud computer.
- **Desktop app.** Claw runs on your own Mac or Windows PC. It needs no Cloud Computer subscription, but it needs your Genspark account and credits. It only works while your computer is on and the app is open.

In both modes, credits are "the fuel for Claw's AI work". The guide says credits are used for every message and tool call, each scheduled task, each Heartbeat check-in and media tasks such as images and video. To get more, you subscribe to a Genspark plan or upgrade.

Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) has no credits. With local models selected, the AI work runs on your computer. You download a model once and use it as much as you like.

## What runs without asking

Genspark's FAQ says external actions such as sending emails or posting messages "require permission". It suggests you start with read-only or low-risk tasks.

Two other notes in the same guide matter:

- **Heartbeat.** When you turn it on, Claw checks your inbox, calendar and other services at a set interval and acts on what needs attention, even when you have not sent a message.
- **Files on the desktop.** The guide says Claw has access to your file system. A workspace folder helps, but it is "soft guidance, not a hard boundary". It tells you to back up important files first.

Off Grid AI starts from your yes. With Pro, **Actions** drafts replies, issues and docs through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion. Each one waits in an approval card with the full text. God follows rules you set, such as "never send anything to Acme without asking". When you use **Computer Use** in an app on screen, you watch the run and can **Pause**, **Stop** or **Take Over**.

## It already knows your day

Claw learns your preferences and context from what you tell it across channels.

Off Grid AI Pro builds its memory from your actual work. Your screens, meetings, mail and documents become one local memory on your disk. Meetings are recorded without a bot and transcribed on your computer. Ask "what did Sam say about the rollout?" and you get the answer with its source.

**God**, your chief of staff, works from that memory: a morning briefing, meeting prep with notes from last time, routines and scheduled tasks, and the list of what people owe you.

## When Genspark Claw is the better pick

Be fair to Claw. Its cloud computer keeps working when your laptop is closed. OGAD works while your computer is on. Claw has 30+ built-in skills, including slides, docs, image and video generation and phone calls. It works inside WhatsApp, LINE, Slack, Teams, Telegram, Discord and more. Off Grid AI does not live in your chat apps.

If you want a cloud worker that runs all night and you are happy to pay in credits, Claw fits. If your mail, meetings and files carry client, legal or deal details you would rather keep on your own machine, use Off Grid AI.

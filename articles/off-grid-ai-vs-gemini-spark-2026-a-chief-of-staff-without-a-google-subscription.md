## What Spark keeps in your Google Account

Google's [help page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507?hl=en) is clear about what stays on its side:

- Keep Activity must be on to use Spark.
- Remote browser data, such as sign-in cookies, is saved for future sessions. You can delete it in settings.
- Files from remote code runs are also saved until you delete them.

None of this is hidden. It is how a cloud agent works. Your context has to live where the agent runs.

## What God does on your computer

God is the chief of staff inside OGAD Pro. It reads your connected Google or Microsoft account and the work already saved in OGAD:

- **A morning brief.** Your next meeting, the decision it needs, and the open task that blocks it. Each fact has a source.

## When Gemini Spark is the better pick

Be fair to yourself. Spark keeps working after you close the laptop. God's routines run inside OGAD, so your computer must be awake and the app open.

Spark also needs no strong hardware. It runs on Google's servers with Gemini 3.5. OGAD runs best on an Apple silicon Mac or a recent PC that fits a good local model. The app shows which models fit before you download.

And if your whole day is in Gmail, Docs and Sheets, Spark's links into those apps are deep. If you already pay for Google AI Pro or Ultra and live outside the excluded regions, try it. If you want your work history on your own machine, with no activity setting to keep on, run OGAD.

## FAQ

**What do I need to use Gemini Spark?**
Google's help page lists a personal Google Account, age 18 or over, and a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription. Keep Activity must be on, and you must opt in to Personal Intelligence. Work and school accounts are not supported yet.

**Is Gemini Spark available in Europe or the UK?**
No. Google's help page says Spark is available where Gemini Apps are supported, except the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland and the United Kingdom.

**Does Gemini Spark ask before it acts?**
For many actions, yes. Google says Gemini confirms before it sends messages, changes or deletes data, makes purchases or submits web forms. It can make bulk changes to private Google Tasks without confirmation.

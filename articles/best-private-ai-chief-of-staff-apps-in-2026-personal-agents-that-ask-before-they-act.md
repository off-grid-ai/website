## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

**What it is.** Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) is free and open source (AGPL-3.0) for Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Pro adds God, a chief of staff: a morning brief, meeting prep with notes from last time, and the to-dos people owe you. It works from a local Memory of your screens, meetings, mail and documents. Actions draft replies and issues in Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, and each one waits for your yes.

**Best for.** Client, legal or deal work you want on your own disk, with a log of every action you approved.

**Price.** $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. Up to 5 devices. No account.

**The catch.** God's routines need the computer awake and the app open. It doesn't live in WhatsApp or Slack, and it needs a computer that fits a good local model; the app shows which fit.

Pro also includes Meetings without a bot, Reflect, Vault, Clipboard, Sync and dictation.

## 2. Hermes Agent

**What it is.** A free, MIT-licensed agent from Nous Research. It writes and reuses its own skills, spawns subagents, and talks to you in Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal and email.

**Best for.** Developers who want a scriptable agent on their own models or a server.

**Price.** Free; Nous Portal is $0, $20, $100 or $200 a month.

**The catch.** Settings live in `config.yaml` and `.env`, and its approval mode lets a second model wave through low-risk commands. See [Off Grid AI vs Hermes Agent](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-hermes-agent-2026-a-chief-of-staff-that-runs-on-your-own-models/).

## 3. Claude Cowork

**What it is.** Anthropic's agent for long, finished work: decks, reports, spreadsheets and designs.

**Best for.** Polished files every day, from a frontier Claude model, steered from web and mobile.

**Price.** Claude Pro $20 a month or $200 a year; Max from $100 a month.

**The catch.** Since 6 October 2026, new Pro and Max tasks run on Anthropic's servers, and Auto mode is the default. See [Off Grid AI vs Claude Cowork](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-claude-cowork-2026-tasks-you-approve-with-memory-on-your-own-computer/).

## 4. Gemini Spark

**What it is.** Google's agent in the Gemini app, running on Gemini 3.5 in Google's cloud.

**Best for.** People whose day is in Gmail, Docs and Sheets.

**Price.** Needs Google AI Pro or Ultra. We could not confirm one current US price.

**The catch.** Keep Activity must stay on, personal accounts only, and it isn't offered in the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland or the UK. See [Off Grid AI vs Gemini Spark](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-gemini-spark-2026-a-chief-of-staff-without-a-google-subscription/).

## 5. OpenClaw

**What it is.** A free, MIT-licensed open agent run by the OpenClaw Foundation, with 29 chat channels, cron jobs and webhooks.

**Best for.** Terminal users who want to message their agent from WhatsApp or Signal.

**Price.** Free; model fees are separate.

**The catch.** Setup needs a terminal and an API key or local runtime, and host commands run without prompts on gateway hosts by default. See [Off Grid AI vs OpenClaw](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-openclaw-2026-a-personal-agent-with-no-setup-that-asks-before-it-acts/).

## 6. Lindy

**What it is.** A cloud assistant for inbox, calendar and meetings, reachable in Slack, iMessage and the browser.

**Best for.** Teams that want one shared assistant across many apps.

**Price.** Team from $49.99 a month per active user.

**The catch.** Your mail and calls are handled in Lindy's cloud. See [Off Grid AI vs Lindy](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-lindy-2026-an-ai-assistant-for-email-and-meetings-that-asks-before-it-acts/).

## 7. Meta Muse

**What it is.** Meta's personal agent on a cloud machine per person, reachable by phone, web or WhatsApp. It can shop for you.

**Best for.** A free helper for errands and shopping.

**Price.** Free for most uses; Power $20 a month; Max $100 a month.

**The catch.** Your files and service logins sit on a machine Meta runs, and it is a US rollout in limited testing. See [Off Grid AI vs Meta Muse](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-meta-muse-2026-a-personal-agent-without-a-cloud-computer/).

## 8. OpenAI dots

**What it is.** OpenAI's agent on its own cloud computer and browser, using GPT-6 Astra.

**Best for.** ChatGPT Pro users who want an agent that works all night.

**Price.** Comes with ChatGPT Pro, from $100 a month.

**The catch.** Automatic checks decide whether an action proceeds or needs your approval. Pro isn't offered in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. See [Off Grid AI vs OpenAI dots](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-openai-dots-2026-a-chief-of-staff-that-runs-on-your-own-computer/).

## 9. Genspark Claw

**What it is.** A cloud AI worker with 30+ skills, inside WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, Telegram and more.

**Best for.** Overnight cloud work, if you're happy paying in credits.

**Price.** "Starting from $9.99/mo" for a limited time, plus credits for every message and tool call.

**The catch.** Costs scale with use, and work runs on Genspark's computer. See [Off Grid AI vs Genspark Claw](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-genspark-claw-2026-an-ai-employee-without-credits-or-a-cloud-computer/).

Not ranked: Microsoft Copilot Autopilot is in private preview with no public price. See [Off Grid AI vs Copilot Autopilot](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-microsoft-copilot-autopilot-2026-a-personal-agent-you-own-outside-your-employers-cloud/).

- **You want a chief of staff on your own computer that asks first, every time:** Off Grid AI Pro.

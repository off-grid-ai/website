## Your words in someone else's cloud

Plaud's pricing page lists "Cloud storage with access across Plaud App, Web, and Desktop". It also names the models it uses, including GPT-5, Gemini 3 Pro and Claude Sonnet. So a doctor's visit, a client pitch or a board talk recorded on a Plaud is handled by Plaud's service and the outside AI models it names.

With OGAD and local models selected, a Whisper model on your computer writes the transcript. A local text model writes the summary, the decisions and the follow-ups. Nothing is uploaded, and it works with Wi-Fi off.

## Recording a room with OGAD

1. Open **Meetings** and press **Record meeting**.
2. Put the laptop where its microphone can hear everyone.
3. Press **Stop** at the end. The transcript and summary appear on your computer.

## When Plaud is the better pick

A Plaud is small and always with you. It records a walk-and-talk, a site visit or a chat over coffee with no laptop open. OGAD needs a computer in the room.

If you meet mostly away from a desk, a wearable recorder is the easier tool. If your meetings happen on calls or around a table with your laptop, OGAD does the job with no device to buy and no minutes to count.

## FAQ

**How much does a Plaud recorder cost?**
On 30 October 2026, Plaud lists the NotePin and the Plaud Note at $159 each in the US store. Each includes the free Starter plan with 300 transcription minutes a month.

**Do I need a Plaud subscription?**
Not to start. Plaud is free for up to 300 minutes a month. Pro is $17.99 a month or $99.99 a year for 1,200 minutes, and Unlimited is $29.99 a month or $239.99 a year.

**Where does Plaud process my recordings?**
Plaud's pricing page lists cloud storage across its app, web and desktop, and AI models including GPT-5, Gemini 3 Pro and Claude Sonnet. Off Grid AI transcribes and summarises on your own computer with local models.

**Can Off Grid AI record an in-person meeting?**
Yes. Press Record meeting in OGAD and your laptop's microphone records the room. The transcript and summary are written on the computer.

**Is there a minute limit in Off Grid AI?**
No. Transcription runs on your own computer, so the only limit is your disk space.

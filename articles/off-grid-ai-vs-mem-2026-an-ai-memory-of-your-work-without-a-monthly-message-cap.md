## How Memory works

1. Turn on what Off Grid AI may remember: screen capture, meetings, mail and documents.
2. A local model reads each item on your computer and notes the people, projects and decisions.
3. Ask a question in chat, such as "what did Sam say about the rollout?"
4. The answer comes with its source. Open **Why** to see the screen, call or mail behind it.

You choose what goes in. You can exclude apps from capture and delete anything.

## More than a notes app

Mem keeps your notes. Off Grid AI Pro also acts on them:

- **Meetings.** Calls recorded without a bot, transcribed and summarised on your computer.
- **Actions you approve.** Replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, sent only with your yes.
- **God, your chief of staff.** A morning brief, routines, meeting prep and what people owe you.
- **Reflect.** Where your day went, by project, app and person.
- **Vault, Clipboard and Sync.** Encrypted secrets, a searchable clipboard, and your phone and computer in sync, device to device.

One key covers up to 5 devices.

## When Mem is the better pick

Mem is a notes app first. It is a good place to write and organise notes, and you can message it over WhatsApp and email. OGAD is not a notes app first, and it needs a computer that can run a local model. Most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can.

If you want a polished notes app with an assistant inside, Mem fits. If you want a memory of everything you worked on, kept on your own devices with no cap, use OGAD.

## FAQ

**How much does Mem cost?**
Mem's pricing page lists Plus at $9 a month and Pro at $29 a month, with larger Pro tiers up to $199 a month. Each plan caps messages and new notes per month.

**Does Off Grid AI limit how many questions I can ask?**
No. Off Grid AI runs local models on your computer, so there is no message cap and no credits.

**Is there a Mem alternative for Linux and Android?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Desktop runs on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux, and the mobile app runs on iPhone and Android. Mem's download page lists iOS, Mac and Windows.

**Does Mem train AI on my notes?**
Mem's privacy policy says it does not use your notes to train generalized AI models. Off Grid AI runs its models on your own computer.

**What goes into Off Grid AI's memory?**
Your screens, meetings, mail and documents, once you turn each one on. It all stays on your own devices, and answers link to the source.

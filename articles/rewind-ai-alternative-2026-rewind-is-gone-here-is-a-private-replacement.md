## How OGAD remembers your day

1. Open **Replay** and choose **Resume capture**. The status shows **Capturing**. Use **Pause capture** whenever you want.
2. OGAD saves moments from your screen as you work. These are sampled frames, not a video of every second.
3. A local vision model reads each moment and writes a short note: what you were doing, in which app, about which project or person.
4. On Windows and Linux you can choose **Overnight**, so the analysis runs while you sleep and your computer stays fast all day.

You can exclude apps you never want saved, edit or delete moments, and clear everything. See [how to delete saved screen activity](/articles/how-to-delete-saved-screen-activity-and-ai-work-notes-in-off-grid-ai-on-mac-in-2026/).

## What you can do with it

- **Find the page you saw.** "The pricing sheet from Tuesday" brings back the moment, with a picture of the screen.

- **See your day.** The **Day** view shows a journal, a timeline and the to-dos you picked up.

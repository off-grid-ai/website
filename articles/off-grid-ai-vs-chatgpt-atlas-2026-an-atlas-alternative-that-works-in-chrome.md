## What to do with your Atlas data

OpenAI's retirement page covers the steps. In short:

- **Bookmarks.** Import a bookmarks HTML file you exported earlier into Chrome.
- **Passwords.** Import a password CSV you exported earlier, then delete the CSV. It holds readable passwords.
- **Sessions.** Sign in to your websites again in Chrome.
- **Chats.** Your ChatGPT conversations are separate from Atlas, and they stay in ChatGPT.

OpenAI also warns that deleting your ChatGPT chats does not delete Atlas browser memories. Use its privacy portal for those.

Off Grid AI can hold your passwords too. The Vault keeps them encrypted on your disk and fills logins in Chrome. See [how to fill website logins from your Off Grid AI Vault](/articles/how-to-fill-website-logins-from-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/).

## How a browser task runs on your computer

1. Install the [Off Grid AI extension](/browser-extension/) in Chrome and keep OGAD running.
2. In the extension, open **Settings > Desktop** and choose **Pair with desktop**. Check that the six words match, then approve in OGAD.
3. Ask about the page you have open. Plain chat gets read-only tools, so it reads and answers.
4. Turn on **Agent** for a task that needs clicks across pages. It uses the sessions you already have.
5. When the task wants to send, create or delete something, OGAD shows the action. You approve it or reject it.

The extension refuses one-time codes, card numbers and CAPTCHAs in code. Sign-ins come from your Vault or from you. For a full walk-through, see [how to automate tasks in your signed-in browser with local AI](/articles/how-to-automate-tasks-in-your-signed-in-browser-with-local-ai-in-2026/).

## When ChatGPT is the better pick

If you want OpenAI's largest models, keep using ChatGPT itself. Your conversations are still there. A big cloud model can be stronger than a local one on hard, open-ended work, and it runs on any laptop. OGAD needs a computer that can run a local model. Most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which models fit.

Off Grid AI's extension is on Chrome today. Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox have a waitlist. If your browser holds client, legal or deal pages, keep the model on your own machine.

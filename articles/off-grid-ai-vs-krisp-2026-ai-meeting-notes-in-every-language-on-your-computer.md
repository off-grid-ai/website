## The language gap

If every call you take is in English, Krisp's on-device transcription keeps the words local. Many teams don't work that way. A sales call in Spanish, a stand-up in German or a client in Korean falls into Krisp's server-based list.

OGAD has no such split. Pick a multilingual Whisper model in **Models**, and every call is transcribed on your computer. See [meeting transcripts in multiple languages on a Mac](/articles/how-to-get-meeting-transcripts-in-multiple-languages-on-your-mac-in-2026-no-cloud-uploads/).

## What you get after the call

When you press **Stop**, a local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model then writes:

- a summary of the call,
- the decisions made,
- the follow-ups, with who owes what,
- the screens that were shared.

Those follow-ups don't sit in a list. Pro can draft the reply in Gmail or the issue in Linear, Jira or Notion, and it runs only when you approve it.

## Meetings are one part of Pro

- **Memory.** Screens, meetings, mail and documents in one local memory you can search and ask.
- **God, your chief of staff.** A morning brief, meeting prep with last time's notes, and what people owe you.
- **Dictation.** Speak into any app on your computer, transcribed locally.

## When Krisp is the better pick

Krisp removes background noise, voices and echo from your calls, in both directions. OGAD doesn't do that at all. Krisp also has mobile apps and team admin on its higher plans.

If a noisy room is your main problem, Krisp is the right tool. If you want private notes in any language, with no seat fee, use OGAD. You can also run both: Krisp for clean audio, OGAD for the notes.

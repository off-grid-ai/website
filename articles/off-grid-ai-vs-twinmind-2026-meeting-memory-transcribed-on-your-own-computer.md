## Four questions to ask any memory app

1. **Capture.** What does it save, and when?
2. **Storage.** Where does the history live?
3. **Inference.** Which model reads it, and where does that model run?
4. **Actions.** Can it do work for you, and who approves it?

## Where the two really differ

**Inference.** TwinMind sends your conversations to cloud AI and speech-to-text providers. Its policy says they "generally delete it within 30 days," and it uses zero data retention where available. With local models selected, OGAD transcribes and summarises on your computer. Nothing goes to a provider.

**Storage.** TwinMind keeps your transcripts in its cloud until you delete them. Its iOS app can keep notes on the device when Backup Notes is off. OGAD keeps everything on your disk on every platform.

**Scope.** TwinMind remembers what was said. OGAD also remembers what was on your screen, so you can ask about the slide, the doc or the ticket from the call.

## How OGAD handles a meeting

1. Join a supported call, such as the Zoom desktop app. OGAD detects it and records on your computer, with a visible indicator and a Stop control. No bot joins the call. Make sure the people in the call agree first.
2. When you stop and save, a local model writes the transcript and the summary, with decisions and follow-ups.

## When TwinMind is the better pick

TwinMind follows you away from your desk. Its phone app captures in-person conversations and calls, and it has an Apple Watch app and a Chrome extension. Its free plan has unlimited transcription, summaries and questions. Because its AI runs in the cloud, it works on any device.

If most of your important talks happen on the move, TwinMind fits well. If your meetings happen at your computer and you want them transcribed there, with your screen memory beside them, use OGAD.

## FAQ

**Where does TwinMind store my transcripts?**
TwinMind's privacy policy says transcripts, summaries and metadata are stored in its cloud on AWS, GCP, Vercel and Neon. On iOS, turning Backup Notes off keeps transcripts on the device only.

**Which AI companies process TwinMind data?**
Its privacy policy names OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, ElevenLabs and Fireworks AI for AI and speech-to-text. It says providers generally delete the data within 30 days.

**Does TwinMind save my audio?**
Not by default. TwinMind transcribes the audio and then deletes it, unless you turn on Save Audio.

**How much does TwinMind cost compared with Off Grid AI?**

**Does Off Grid AI join my calls as a bot?**
No. Off Grid AI records the meeting on your computer without a bot and transcribes it with a local model.

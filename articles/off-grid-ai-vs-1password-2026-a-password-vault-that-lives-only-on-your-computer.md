## How Vault works

1. Open **Vault** in OGAD and set a master password.
2. Save the 24-word recovery phrase somewhere safe. With the vault file and its recovery data, it lets you back in if you forget the password.
3. Add logins, payment cards, API keys, secure notes and files. Everything is encrypted on your disk.
4. Lock it when you finish. Only you can unlock it.
5. To fill logins in Chrome, [request the browser extension](/articles/how-to-fill-website-logins-from-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) and pair it with the desktop app.

The extension can also fill [payment card details](/articles/how-to-fill-payment-card-details-from-your-local-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) on supported forms, for you to review. Run the [Security check](/articles/how-to-find-weak-and-reused-passwords-in-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) to find weak and reused passwords.

## When 1Password is the better pick

For most families, 1Password is the better password manager. It shares vaults across 5 people with admin controls. Autofill and autosave of logins come with every plan. It has Watchtower alerts for compromised passwords and Travel Mode for crossing borders. Its [pricing page](https://1password.com/pricing) lists downloads for macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, Linux, the browser and the command line. Off Grid AI Vault does not match that reach.

If you share passwords with family or a team, keep 1Password. If you want your own secrets in a vault that no company holds, next to your API keys and private files, use Off Grid AI Vault.

## FAQ

**How much does 1Password cost?**
1Password Individual is $3.99 a month billed yearly, and Families is $5.99 a month for 5 people. On 30 October 2026 new customers paying yearly get $2.99 and $4.49 for the first year.

**Do I need an account for Off Grid AI Vault?**
No. Vault lives in Off Grid AI Desktop on your computer. You unlock it with a master password, and you can recover it with a 24-word recovery phrase.

**Can Off Grid AI fill passwords in my browser?**
Yes, in Chrome, through the Off Grid AI browser extension, which is available by request. The desktop app must be running, paired and unlocked. 1Password's autofill is more complete and works across more places.

**Can I share passwords with my family in Off Grid AI?**
Off Grid AI Pro has no family plan; one key covers up to 5 of your own devices. For shared family vaults, 1Password Families is the better pick.

**Does Off Grid AI find weak or reused passwords?**
Yes. The Security check in the browser Vault lists weak, repeated and older passwords stored in your local Vault, so you can change them.

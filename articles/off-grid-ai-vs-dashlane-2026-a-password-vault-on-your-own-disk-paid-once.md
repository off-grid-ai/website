## How Vault works

1. Open **Vault** in OGAD and set a master password.
2. Save the 24-word recovery phrase somewhere safe. With the vault file and its recovery data, it lets you back in if you forget the password.
3. Add logins, payment cards, API keys, secure notes and files. Everything is encrypted on your disk.
4. Lock it when you finish. Only you can unlock it.
5. To fill logins in Chrome, [request the browser extension](/articles/how-to-fill-website-logins-from-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) and pair it with the desktop app.

The extension can also fill [payment card details](/articles/how-to-fill-payment-card-details-from-your-local-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) on supported forms, for you to review. Run the [Security check](/articles/how-to-find-weak-and-reused-passwords-in-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) to find weak and reused passwords.

## Your vault works with your AI agent

Off Grid Vault fills logins in your own browser, matched to the website's domain. Off Grid's [Web Use](/articles/how-to-automate-tasks-in-your-signed-in-browser-with-local-ai-in-2026/) runs agent tasks in that same signed-in browser. So an agent task can sign in without you pasting a password into a chat, and the password never goes to a model. Web Use and Computer Use are Pro features.

## When Dashlane is the better pick

For families and phone users, Dashlane is the better password manager. Friends & Family covers 10 members, each with a private account. Sharing is unlimited. It offers apps for iOS, macOS and Android, plus a browser extension. It saves and signs in with [passkeys](https://support.dashlane.com/hc/en-us/articles/7888558064274-Passkeys-in-Dashlane). It adds phishing alerts, dark web monitoring and a VPN. Off Grid AI Vault does not fill passwords on your phone and has no family plan.

If you share logins with family or need autofill on your phone, keep Dashlane. If you want your own secrets on your own disk, with no subscription, next to your API keys and private files, use Off Grid AI Vault.

## FAQ

**How much does Dashlane cost?**
On 30 October 2026, Dashlane lists Premium at $5.42 a month and Friends & Family at $8.13 a month for 10 members, both billed yearly. Its personal pricing page offers a 14-day free trial and lists no free plan.

**Where does Dashlane store my passwords?**
Dashlane says your data is encrypted, sent from your app to its servers and stored there, then downloaded to each device where you log in. It is backed up daily across multiple server instances.

**Do I need an account for Off Grid AI Vault?**
No. Vault lives in Off Grid AI Desktop on your computer. You unlock it with a master password, and you can recover it with a 24-word recovery phrase.

**Can Off Grid AI fill passwords in my browser?**
Yes, in Chrome, through the Off Grid AI browser extension, which is available by request. The desktop app must be running, paired and unlocked. Dashlane's autofill is more complete and works on phones too.

**Can I share passwords with my family in Off Grid AI?**
Off Grid AI Pro has no family plan; one key covers up to 5 of your own devices. For shared family logins, Dashlane Friends & Family is the better pick.

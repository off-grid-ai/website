## How Off Grid AI reads your tabs on your computer

1. Install the [Off Grid AI extension](/browser-extension/) in Chrome and keep OGAD running.
2. In the extension, open **Settings > Desktop** and choose **Pair with desktop**. Check that the six words match, then approve in OGAD.
3. Ask about the page you have open. Plain chat gets read-only tools, so it reads and answers.
4. Turn on **Agent** when the work spans several pages. It uses the sessions you are already signed in to.
5. When a task wants to send, create or delete something, OGAD shows the action. You approve it or reject it.

The page goes to the model you select in OGAD. Pick a local model and the page stays on your computer. See [how to ask AI about private work websites in Chrome](/articles/how-to-ask-ai-about-private-work-websites-in-chrome-in-2026-without-cloud-ai/).

## When Dia is the better pick

Dia is a polished browser with its own design, tab tools and work features. It connects to Slack, Notion and Teams, and Dia for Work adds SSO and admin tools. Its sync is end-to-end encrypted. It also runs on any laptop, because the AI runs on its partners' servers. OGAD needs a computer that can run a local model. Most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which models fit.

If you like Dia and your company is happy with its AI partners, keep it. If your tabs hold client, legal or deal details, read them with a model on your own machine. Off Grid AI's extension is on Chrome today; Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox have a waitlist.

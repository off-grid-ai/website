## The bot, and the bot-free mode

By default, Fireflies sends its Notetaker bot into your Zoom, Meet or Teams call. Fireflies now has a desktop app for Mac and Windows that records system audio without the bot. Its [help guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6666374717-how-to-record-meetings-without-a-bot-on-the-fireflies-desktop-app) says that mode "does not save audio or video files". You get the transcript and summary in your Fireflies account.

OGAD never joins the call either. It sees that a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call has started and records two tracks: the call's audio and your microphone. For a meeting in a room, press **Record meeting**. The difference is where the work happens. With local models, the recording never leaves your computer.

## What you get after the call

When you stop, a local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model then writes:

- a summary of the call,
- the decisions made,
- the follow-ups, with who owes what.

Fireflies counts AI work in credits; its pricing page lists 20 AI credits on the free and Pro plans. OGAD has no credits. You can ask about a call as often as you like, because the model runs on your hardware.

## When Fireflies is the better pick

Fireflies is built for teams. It has shared channels, team analytics, conversation intelligence and a large set of integrations. It records on any laptop because its servers do the work, and it has phone apps. OGAD needs a computer that can run a speech model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which models fit.

If your sales team needs shared call libraries and CRM sync, Fireflies does that well. If you want your own calls private and paid for once, use OGAD.

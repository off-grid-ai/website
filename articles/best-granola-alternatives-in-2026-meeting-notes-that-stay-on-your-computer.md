## Why people leave Granola

Granola got the hard part right. It listens to your computer's audio, so nobody sees a bot. But its [security page](https://www.granola.ai/security) says transcription runs on Deepgram and AssemblyAI, summaries go to third parties such as OpenAI or Anthropic, and notes are stored on US-hosted AWS. The free plan keeps 30 days of history, and Business is $14 per user a month. It runs on macOS and Windows, not Linux.

## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

**What it is.** Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) is free and open source (AGPL-3.0) for Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Pro records Zoom, Teams and Google Meet calls without a bot, on two tracks: their audio and your microphone. A local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model writes the summary, decisions and follow-ups. Every meeting stays on your disk for as long as you keep it.

**Best for.** Granola users who want the same quiet recording with no AI company in the loop, and anyone on Linux.

**Price.** $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. Up to 5 devices. No account.

**The catch.** No shared Team Spaces like Granola's. Your computer must run a speech model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which fit. See [Off Grid AI vs Granola](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-granola-2026-meeting-notes-that-never-leave-your-computer/).

Pro is more than a notepad. The same price adds Memory of your screens, mail and documents, Actions you approve in Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, God for a morning brief and meeting prep, Reflect, Vault, Clipboard, Sync and dictation.

## 2. MacWhisper

**What it is.** A local transcription app for the Mac. It records Zoom, Teams, Webex, Skype and Discord without a bot.

**Best for.** Mac users who want transcripts and nothing else.

**Price.** Free version; Pro €64 once.

**The catch.** Mac only, and no memory or follow-up list. See [Off Grid AI vs MacWhisper](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-macwhisper-2026-local-transcription-on-every-computer-not-just-mac/).

## 3. Krisp

**What it is.** Noise cancellation plus meeting notes. No bot by default, and English is transcribed on your device.

**Best for.** Calls from noisy places. It cleans audio both ways.

**Price.** Core $16 a month, or $8 a month billed annually.

**The catch.** 15 other languages are server-based. See [Off Grid AI vs Krisp](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-krisp-2026-ai-meeting-notes-in-every-language-on-your-computer/).

## 4. TwinMind

**What it is.** A bot-free meeting memory on computer, phone, Chrome and Apple Watch.

**Best for.** A free Granola swap, if the cloud is fine. Its free plan has unlimited transcription.

**Price.** Free; Pro $11.99 a month and Max $29.99 a month, billed yearly.

**The catch.** It uses OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others for AI and speech-to-text. See [Off Grid AI vs TwinMind](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-twinmind-2026-meeting-memory-transcribed-on-your-own-computer/).

## 5. Notion AI Meeting Notes

**What it is.** Bot-free notes inside Notion's desktop app.

**Best for.** Teams already on Notion Business, where notes become shared pages.

**Price.** Business, $20 per member a month.

**The catch.** Business plan only, 10 hours a day, no offline use. See [Off Grid AI vs Notion AI Meeting Notes](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-notion-ai-meeting-notes-2026-no-business-plan-no-cloud-ai/).

## 6. Fireflies

**What it is.** A team notetaker. It sends a bot by default, but its desktop app records without one.

**Best for.** Sales teams that want shared call libraries, analytics and CRM sync.

**Price.** Pro $10 a seat a month billed yearly, or $18 monthly.

**The catch.** The free plan holds 400 minutes per team and limits AI summaries. See [Off Grid AI vs Fireflies](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-fireflies-2026-meeting-notes-with-no-bot-and-no-storage-cap/).

## 7. Bluedot

**What it is.** A bot-free recorder as a Chrome extension or app, with phone apps.

**Best for.** Locked-down computers where only an extension is allowed.

**Price.** Basic $14 and Pro $20 per user a month, billed annually.

**The catch.** 5 free meetings in total, and recordings in AWS. See [Off Grid AI vs Bluedot](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-bluedot-2026-unlimited-meeting-notes-that-stay-on-your-computer/).

## 8. Jamie

**What it is.** A bot-free notetaker with an iPhone app and notes kept in Europe.

**Best for.** In-person meetings captured on a phone, and CRM links on Pro.

**Price.** 10 free notes a month; Pro €39 a month, billed annually.

**The catch.** Audio is streamed for transcription, and Pro costs the most here. See [Off Grid AI vs Jamie](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-jamie-2026-bot-free-meeting-notes-without-the-cloud/).

Perplexity Portable Computer puts an agent on your own hardware. Perplexity's [launch post](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-portable-computer-for-local-first-ai) says it reads local files, runs multi-step work, and asks before it sends content to the cloud. Local work uses no Computer credits.

The catch is the hardware and the plan. You need Perplexity Pro or Max, and [at least 24 GB of VRAM](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/portable-computer-for-windows-is-here) on a supported GPU.

Off Grid AI runs on the computer you already have. **It picks models that fit your memory, remembers your work, and acts only after your yes.** Pro costs $79.99 once.

## The hardware question

Perplexity's posts set a clear floor. On Windows, you need an eligible NVIDIA GeForce RTX or RTX PRO card with at least 24 GB of VRAM. On AMD, you need a [Ryzen AI Max system](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/portable-computer-comes-to-amd-powered-agentic-pcs) with at least 24 GB of GPU-accessible memory, on Windows 10 or 11. The first release ran on NVIDIA DGX Spark with Linux.

Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) has no floor like that. The **Models** screen shows which models fit your computer before you download. A 16 GB laptop runs a small model. A big desktop runs a big one. Most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can run a useful model today.

## When Perplexity Portable Computer is the better pick

Pick Portable Computer if you already pay for Perplexity Pro or Max and own a 24 GB GPU or a DGX Spark. You get Perplexity Search and access to more than 15 frontier models when a task needs them, with a permission step first. Its Windows post also lists scheduled local tasks and connectors for Gmail, Outlook, Slack and GitHub.

Pick Off Grid if you use a Mac, have an ordinary laptop, want a memory of your meetings and mail, or don't want a monthly plan.

## FAQ

**What is Perplexity Portable Computer?**
It is the local version of Perplexity's Computer agent. Perplexity says it runs the model, agent harness, orchestrator and scheduler on your device, and asks permission before it sends anything to the cloud.

**What hardware does Perplexity Portable Computer need?**
Perplexity's posts list NVIDIA DGX Spark on Linux, Windows PCs with an eligible NVIDIA RTX GPU and at least 24 GB of VRAM, and Windows PCs with AMD Ryzen AI Max and at least 24 GB of GPU-accessible memory.

**Does Perplexity Portable Computer work on a Mac?**
Perplexity's Portable Computer posts do not mention macOS. Off Grid AI Desktop runs on Apple silicon Macs, Windows and Linux.

**How much does Perplexity Portable Computer cost?**
It is part of Perplexity Pro and Max. Perplexity's help center lists Max at $200 a month or $2,000 a year. Off Grid AI Pro costs $79.99 once on 30 October 2026, a limited-time price.

**Does Off Grid AI act on my behalf?**
Only with your approval. Off Grid AI Pro drafts replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, and nothing goes out until you say yes. Computer Use and Web Use tasks run in visible apps you can watch.

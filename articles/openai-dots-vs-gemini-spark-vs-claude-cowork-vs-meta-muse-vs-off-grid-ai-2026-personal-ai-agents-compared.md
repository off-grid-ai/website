## FAQ

**Which personal AI agent keeps my data on my own computer?**
Off Grid AI Desktop Pro. Its memory stays on your disk, and with local models selected the model runs on your computer too. OpenAI dots, Gemini Spark, new Claude Cowork tasks on Pro and Max, and Meta Muse all run in their makers' clouds.

**Which personal AI agent asks before every action?**
Off Grid AI. Every reply, issue or doc waits in Actions for your yes, and each approval is logged. Gemini Spark and Meta Muse confirm before sensitive actions. Claude Cowork asks in Manual mode, but Auto mode is the default. OpenAI dots uses automatic checks to decide.

**Which personal AI agent is cheapest?**
Meta Muse is free for most uses. Among paid options, Off Grid AI Pro is $79.99 once on 30 October 2026, a limited-time price. Claude Pro is $200 a year, and dots comes with ChatGPT Pro from $100 a month.

**Can I use these agents in Europe or the UK?**
Off Grid AI has no region limit and no account. OpenAI's docs say Pro dots are not in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. Google says Spark is not in the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland or the UK. Meta says Muse is rolling out in the US.

**Does Off Grid AI keep working when my computer is off?**
No. God's routines run inside the app, so the computer must be awake and the app open. Dots, Spark, Cowork cloud tasks and Muse run in the cloud, so they keep going without your computer.

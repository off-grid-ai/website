Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) Pro writes its summaries of your day with a local model. **The AI part stays on your computer too.** It also runs on Windows and Linux, and you pay once.

## How Reflect works

1. Turn on capture in **Replay** with **Resume capture**. OGAD saves moments from your screen as you work, on your disk.
2. A local model reads each moment and notes the app, the project and the people involved.
3. Open **Reflect**. You see your day by project, app and person, plus your focus time.
4. Switch to the week view to see where your time goes over several days.

You can exclude apps from capture, pause it, and delete any moment. On Windows and Linux you can run the analysis overnight, so your computer stays fast while you work.

## When Timing is the better pick

Timing is built for people who bill by the hour. It has customizable reports, report rounding, manual entries and a web app. Its Connect plan adds two-way sync with ClickUp, Linear and Clio, GrandTotal invoicing, scripting and team features. Off Grid AI has no billing tools.

If you are on a Mac and bill clients, Timing is the stronger tool, and you can leave its AI summaries off. If you want AI summaries that never leave your computer, or you work on Windows or Linux, use OGAD.

## FAQ

**Does Timing send my data to the cloud?**
Only if you use Timing Sync or its AI summaries. Timing's privacy policy says Sync stores copies of all your Timing data, not end-to-end encrypted. AI summaries use Google's Vertex AI in the EU, which may receive the window titles of the apps you use.

**How much does Timing cost?**
Timing Professional is $9 a month billed annually, or $11 monthly. Expert, which adds AI activity summaries, is $11 a month billed annually, or $14 monthly.

**Is there a Timing alternative for Windows and Linux?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Desktop Pro shows time by project, app and person on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Timing is for macOS only.

**Where does Off Grid AI write its time summaries?**
On your computer, with a local model. Your screen moments stay on your disk, and nothing is uploaded.

**Does Off Grid AI make invoices or timesheets?**
No. Off Grid AI shows where your time went. For client billing, Timing's reports, rounding and invoicing tools are the better fit.

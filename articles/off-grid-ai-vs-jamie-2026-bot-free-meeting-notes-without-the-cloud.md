## Streamed is not the same as local

Jamie's policy is strong: no stored audio, no training on your data, and it says that is "contractually enforced" with its model providers. It doesn't name those providers on the page.

OGAD takes the other route. With local models selected, nothing is streamed at all. The audio, the transcript and the summary are made and kept on your computer. There is no provider to trust, because there isn't one.

It also means the notes keep working on a plane or behind a strict firewall. The call itself still needs Zoom's or Teams's connection.

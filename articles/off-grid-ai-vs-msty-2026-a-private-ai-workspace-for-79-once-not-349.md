## Where Msty is strong

Msty gives you a lot of model choice. Its [docs](https://docs.msty.ai/studio/managing-models/local-models) say Studio Desktop runs local models through Ollama, MLX and llama.cpp. It also connects hosted providers, and Aurum adds Azure and Bedrock. [Studio](https://msty.ai/studio) has split chats to run models side by side, and personas that can share one thread.

It also runs on more desktop hardware. The Studio page lists Apple silicon and Intel Macs, x64 and ARM64 Windows, and Linux. OGAD's Mac build is for Apple silicon only.

For companies, Msty sells a suite: Go for agents, Nexus as a model gateway and Stack for governed knowledge, which is marked "Coming soon".

## What Off Grid adds: a memory of your day

Msty's Knowledge Stacks answer from files you add. Off Grid Projects do that too, for free. Pro goes one step further. It builds the memory for you, from the work you already do.

- **Memory.** Screens, meetings, mail and documents become one local memory. Ask a question and get the answer with its source.
- **Meetings without a bot.** OGAD records the call's audio and your microphone, then transcribes and summarises on your computer.
- **Actions you approve.** It drafts replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion. Nothing goes out without your yes.
- **God, your chief of staff.** A morning brief, meeting prep with notes from last time, and the to-dos people owe you.
- **Reflect, Vault, Clipboard and Sync.** Where your day went, encrypted passwords and files, a searchable clipboard, and device-to-device sync.

You don't build a knowledge base first. You work, and Off Grid remembers.

## A phone app that runs models itself

Msty's [Go page](https://msty.ai/go) says you can "keep control from desktop or mobile". The mobile role it describes is checking progress and approving decisions. It does not list models that run on the phone.

Off Grid AI Mobile (OGAM) runs its own on-device models on iPhone and Android. It works offline. At home, it can also use the bigger model on your computer. With Pro, Sync carries chats and Projects between them, device to device.

## When Msty is the better pick

Pick Msty if you want many cloud providers and local engines in one window, side-by-side model comparison, or an Intel Mac. Pick it if your company wants a managed suite with SSO, role-based access and an audit log, which Msty lists for Enterprise and Teams.

Pick Off Grid if you want the assistant to know your meetings, mail and screens, act after your yes, and run on your phone, for one lower price.

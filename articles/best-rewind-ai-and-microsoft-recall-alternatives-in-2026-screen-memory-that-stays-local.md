Pro adds Memory: after you choose **Resume capture**, it saves sampled screen moments on your disk, beside your meetings, mail and documents. Ask "what did Sam say about the rollout?" and get an answer with the source. You can exclude apps, pause capture and delete moments.

**Best for.** Rewind users on any OS, and PC owners without a Copilot+ NPU.

**Price.** $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. Up to 5 devices. No account.

**The catch.** Your computer must run a small local vision model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can. God's routines run inside the app, so the computer must be awake. ## 2. LUCI

**What it is.** A free, local screen and meeting memory. It stores encrypted on your disk and reads with local models such as Phi, Qwen, Llama and DeepSeek.

**Best for.** Developers who want Claude Code, Cursor or Codex to see what was on their screen. It also records meeting video and gives live call coaching.

**Price.** Free, with no paid tier.

**The catch.** No Linux, no phone apps listed, and it hands work to other agents rather than doing it. See [Off Grid AI vs LUCI](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-luci-2026-local-memory-that-does-the-work-itself/).

## 3. Screenpipe

**What it is.** Screen, audio, keyboard and app events, saved on your disk when something changes.

**Best for.** Developers who want raw history for their own agents, through a REST API and MCP server.

**Price.** Free with limited history; Basic $21 a month or $250 a year.

**The catch.** Commercial use needs a licence, and full history costs $250 a year. See [Off Grid AI vs Screenpipe](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-screenpipe-2026-local-screen-memory-with-approved-actions-paid-once/).

## 4. Microsoft Recall

**What it is.** Encrypted screen snapshots built into Windows 11, with Windows Hello for every launch.

**Best for.** Anyone who already owns a Copilot+ PC. It costs nothing extra, filters passwords and card numbers by default, and IT can manage it.

**Price.** Included with Windows 11 on a Copilot+ PC.

**The catch.** Copilot+ hardware only, Windows only, and no meeting recording listed. See [Off Grid AI vs Microsoft Recall](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-microsoft-recall-2026-private-screen-memory-on-any-pc-mac-or-linux/).

## 5. Pieces

**What it is.** A developer work memory: focused app, copy and paste, and opt-in meetings, stored on your device.

**Best for.** Developers who want deep IDE links and frontier cloud models.

**Price.** $18.99 per user a month, after a 7-day trial that needs a card.

**The catch.** Its docs say LLM features run in the cloud. See [Off Grid AI vs Pieces](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-pieces-2026-work-memory-read-by-a-model-on-your-computer/).

## 6. Littlebird

**What it is.** A cloud work memory that reads the text of your active window every few seconds.

**Best for.** Any laptop, with phone and web apps to ask questions away from your desk, plus SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA.

**Price.** Free Basic; Plus $17 a month billed yearly.

**The catch.** Memory is stored on AWS and sent to AI providers. It doesn't stay local. See [Off Grid AI vs Littlebird](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-littlebird-2026-a-work-memory-that-stays-off-the-cloud/).

## Where tl;dv is strong

tl;dv takes privacy seriously. Its home page says it will "never, ever use them to train AI", and it stores data in the EU. Its [desktop app](https://tldv.io/desktop-app) for Mac and Windows records system audio, and the page says "no bot joins the call".

So the real difference is not the bot. It is where the work happens and what the free plan holds back. tl;dv's recordings and notes live on its servers, and tl;dv's home page says it partners with Anthropic for its generative AI. The help center also says that under heavy load, "your meeting may not be recorded if you have the free plan".

## What OGAD does instead

OGAD sees that a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call has started and records two tracks on your computer: the call's audio and your microphone. For a meeting in a room, press **Record meeting**.

When you stop, a local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model then writes the summary, the decisions and the follow-ups, with who owes what. Every meeting gets the full notes. There is no 10-meeting allowance and no 10-minute cut-off, because the models run on your hardware.

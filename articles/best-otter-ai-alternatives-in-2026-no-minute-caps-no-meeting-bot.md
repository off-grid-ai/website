## Why people look past Otter

Otter's [pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing) gives the free plan 300 minutes a month and 30 minutes per conversation. Its [privacy and security page](https://otter.ai/privacy-security) says calls are kept on AWS and de-identified data is used for training. OtterPilot joins calls as a bot, though the desktop app can record without one. Pro is $16.99 a month, or $8.33 a month billed yearly.

## 1. Off Grid AI Pro

**What it is.** Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) is free and open source (AGPL-3.0) for Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Pro records Zoom, Teams and Google Meet calls without a bot, plus in-room meetings with **Record meeting**. A local Whisper model writes the transcript, in many languages with a multilingual model. A local text model writes the summary and who owes what.

**Best for.** People who hit Otter's minute cap, and anyone who doesn't want calls used for training.

**Price.** $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price down from $119, or $7.99 a month. Up to 5 devices. No account.

**The catch.** No web app and no shared team workspace. Your computer must run a speech model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can. See [Off Grid AI vs Otter](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-otter-2026-no-meeting-bot-no-minute-limits-no-cloud/).

The same Pro adds Memory, Actions you approve in Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, God for meeting prep, Reflect, Vault, Clipboard, Sync and dictation.

## 2. MacWhisper

**What it is.** A local transcription app for the Mac. It records Zoom, Teams, Webex, Skype and Discord without a bot.

**Best for.** Mac users who want transcripts only.

**Price.** Free version; Pro €64 once.

**The catch.** Mac only, and no summaries into a memory or follow-up list. See [Off Grid AI vs MacWhisper](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-macwhisper-2026-local-transcription-on-every-computer-not-just-mac/).

## 3. Fireflies

**What it is.** A team notetaker with a bot, plus a desktop app that records without one.

**Best for.** Sales teams: shared channels, analytics and many integrations.

**Price.** Pro $10 a seat a month billed yearly, or $18 monthly.

**The catch.** Free storage is 400 minutes per team, and free AI summaries are limited. See [Off Grid AI vs Fireflies](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-fireflies-2026-meeting-notes-with-no-bot-and-no-storage-cap/).

## 4. Read AI

**What it is.** Meeting notes plus engagement and coaching metrics.

**Best for.** Companies that want meeting analytics across a team.

**Price.** Pro $15 a user a month billed yearly, or $19.75 monthly.

**The catch.** The free plan stops at 5 transcripts a month, 1 hour each. See [Off Grid AI vs Read AI](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-read-ai-2026-unlimited-meeting-notes-on-your-own-computer/).

## 5. tl;dv

**What it is.** A team notetaker with clips and CRM links, stored in the EU.

**Best for.** Teams that need EU storage and HubSpot or Salesforce sync.

**Price.** Pro €18 a seat a month billed yearly, or €29 monthly.

**The catch.** Free AI notes cover only your first 10 meetings, free recordings last 3 months, and every plan caps a recording at 3 hours. See [Off Grid AI vs tl;dv](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-tldv-2026-meeting-notes-that-never-expire/).

## 6. Krisp

**What it is.** Noise cancellation with notes. No bot by default, English transcribed on your device.

**Best for.** Noisy rooms.

**Price.** Core $16 a month, or $8 billed annually.

**The catch.** Other languages are server-based, and there is no free plan past the trial. See [Off Grid AI vs Krisp](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-krisp-2026-ai-meeting-notes-in-every-language-on-your-computer/).

## 7. Fathom

**What it is.** A free notetaker that joins calls as a bot.

**Best for.** Free notes on any laptop, with team plans and CRM sync.

**Price.** Free; Premium $20 a month, or $16 billed yearly.

**The catch.** A bot joins by default, and recordings live in Fathom's US cloud. See [Off Grid AI vs Fathom](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-fathom-2026-meeting-notes-without-a-bot-in-the-call/).

## 8. Plaud

**What it is.** A wearable recorder, NotePin or Plaud Note, for $159.

**Best for.** Walk-and-talks and site visits with no laptop.

**Price.** Starter is 300 minutes a month; Pro $99.99 a year; Unlimited $239.99 a year.

**The catch.** A device to buy, and minutes to count. See [Off Grid AI vs Plaud](/articles/off-grid-ai-vs-plaud-2026-no-recorder-to-buy-no-minutes-to-count/).

## Which one should you pick

- **You hit Otter's minute cap and want privacy:** Off Grid AI Pro.

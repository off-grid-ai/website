## What Off Grid AI does before a meeting

Reclaim makes sure the meeting has a slot. Off Grid AI makes sure you walk in ready:

1. **Meeting prep.** Notes from the last meeting with the same people, with the sources behind them.
2. **A morning brief.** Your meetings, to-dos and pending approvals as one local notification.
3. **What people owe you.** To-dos from calls and mail, with owners and where they came from.
4. **Meetings without a bot.** The call is recorded, transcribed and summarised on your computer.
5. **Follow-ups you approve.** Replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, sent only with your yes.

All of this comes from God, your chief of staff in Pro, and from **Memory**: your screens, meetings, mail and documents, searchable on your own devices.

## When Reclaim.ai is the better pick

For scheduling, Reclaim wins outright. It books habits, defends focus time, finds meeting times across attendees, and syncs tasks from Asana, ClickUp, Todoist, Jira and Linear. Off Grid AI does none of that.

If your problem is a calendar that fills up before your real work, use Reclaim. If your problem is walking into meetings without the context, use Off Grid AI. Many people will want both.

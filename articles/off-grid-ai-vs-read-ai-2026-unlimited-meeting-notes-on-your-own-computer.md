## Read in the room, or nothing in the room

Read AI's home page says "Everyone in the meeting knows Read is there." That is honest, and it is also the point. Some clients don't want an AI scoring the call. Read's [desktop app](https://www.read.ai/desktop) can record "no bot necessary", but it still needs a Read AI account, and your meetings go to Read's servers.

OGAD never joins the call. It sees that a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call has started and records two tracks on your computer: the call's audio and your microphone. For a meeting in a room, press **Record meeting**. Nothing new shows up in the participant list.

## Training and retention

Read AI's home page says "no training on your data by default". Its privacy policy adds that it may use information "to train and improve our models within our Services", consistent with your account settings. It also says audio and video are kept for no longer than 2 years.

With OGAD and local models, your meetings never reach a server. You keep a meeting as long as you want and delete it when you want.

## What you get after the call

When you stop, a local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model then writes:

- a summary of the call,
- the decisions made,
- the follow-ups, with who owes what.

Your meetings then join the rest of your Off Grid AI memory. Before your next call with the same client, God's meeting prep shows what you discussed last time and what's still open.

## Meetings are one part of Pro

- **Memory.** Screens, meetings, mail and documents in one local memory you can search and ask.
- **Actions you approve.** Replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion. Nothing runs without your yes.
- **God, your chief of staff.** A morning brief, meeting prep and what people owe you.

OGAD needs a computer that can run a speech model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which models fit.

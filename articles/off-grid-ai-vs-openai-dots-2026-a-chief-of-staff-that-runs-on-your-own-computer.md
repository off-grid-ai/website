## What God does on your computer

God is the chief of staff inside OGAD Pro. It works from what Pro already knows about your day:

- **A morning brief.** Your next meeting, the decision it needs, and the task that blocks it. Each fact has a source.
- **Meeting prep.** Notes from the last meeting with the same people, ready before the call.
- **What people owe you.** Promises from calls and mail, with who said it and when.
- **Routines.** Scheduled tasks, such as a weekday brief or an approvals digest. You set the days and time.
- **Rules.** Instructions God always follows, such as "never send anything to Acme without asking."

When God wants to send a reply or create an issue, the action goes to **Actions** and waits. You read it, then approve or reject it. Every approval is written to a log.

## God is one part of Pro

Dots gets its context from ChatGPT memory and the apps you connect. OGAD Pro builds a fuller picture, on your own disk:

- **Memory.** Your screens, meetings, mail and documents become one local memory you can search and ask, with sources.
- **Meetings.** Calls recorded without a bot, then transcribed and summarised on your computer.
- **Actions you approve.** Replies, issues and docs through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion.
- **Reflect.** Your time by project, app and person. No timers.
- **Vault, Clipboard and Sync.** Encrypted secrets, a searchable clipboard, and your devices in sync, device to device. No server keeps a copy.

All of it is in the same $79.99, on up to 5 devices.

## Where your data goes

With local models selected, OGAD reads your memory and writes the brief on your computer. No model we host sees it, because we host no model.

Some steps still use the network. Reading Gmail needs Gmail. Creating a Linear issue needs Linear. The difference is that the reasoning and the memory stay with you. If you pick a remote model, the context for that answer goes to that provider. The app shows which model is active.

## When OpenAI dots is the better pick

Be fair to yourself. Dots works while your laptop is closed. God's routines run inside the app, so your computer must be awake and OGAD open. If you need an agent that runs all night with no machine of your own, dots does that.

Dots also needs no hardware. It runs on OpenAI's servers with a large frontier model. OGAD runs best on an Apple silicon Mac or a recent PC with enough memory for a good local model. The app shows which models fit before you download.

If you already pay for ChatGPT Pro and use it every day, try dots first. If your day holds client, legal or deal details you'd rather keep on your own machine, run your chief of staff there.

## Locked to one maker

Lenovo's product news of 3 September 2026 says Qira now runs on "dozens of 16GB RAM Lenovo PCs", such as the Yoga 7 2-in-1 and IdeaPad Slim 5. On phones, it came to the motorola edge 70 max in July, and it is expanding to eligible Motorola devices as they get Android 17.

That is a good list if you already own those devices. If your laptop is a MacBook, a Dell or a home-built Linux box, Qira is not there. If your phone is an iPhone, Lenovo lists no Qira app for it.

Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) runs on Mac, Windows and Linux. Off Grid AI Mobile (OGAM) runs on iPhone and Android, with on-device models for chat, voice, vision and images. One Pro key covers up to 5 devices, from any maker.

## Local first, but how local?

Lenovo says "Your personal data is stored on your device", connecting to the cloud when you need it. It also says Qira can chat with "no connection necessary". That is a good start. But its legal note says certain features "may require cloud processing", and some may share limited data with third-party providers. Its connectors to Gmail, Outlook and Slack run through Workato.

With Off Grid AI, you pick the model and it runs on your hardware. Turn off Wi-Fi and it still answers. Sync goes device to device over your own network. See [how to pair your phone and computer](/articles/how-to-pair-your-phone-and-computer-in-off-grid-ai-in-2026-local-chat-and-file-sync/).

## When Qira is the better pick

If you already use a Lenovo PC and a Motorola phone, Qira is built in. Say "Hey Qira" and it answers. It can look at your screen or camera for live help, summarize your notifications, create images and documents, and connect to services such as Notion and Perplexity. In the United States it also plans trips with Expedia and shops with Instacart. Off Grid AI is an app you install, and it does not have those shopping and travel connectors.

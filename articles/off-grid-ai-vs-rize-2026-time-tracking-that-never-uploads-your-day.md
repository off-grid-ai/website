## How Reflect works

1. Turn on capture in **Replay** with **Resume capture**. OGAD saves moments from your screen as you work, on your disk.
2. A local model reads each moment and notes the app, the project and the people involved.
3. Open **Reflect**. You see your day and week by project, app and person, plus your focus time.
4. Switch to the week view to see where your time goes over several days.

On Windows and Linux you can run the analysis overnight, so your computer stays fast while you work. You can exclude apps from capture and delete any moment.

Rize says it doesn't store screenshots. OGAD does keep screen moments, but only on your disk, and you can open any saved moment again in **Replay**.

## When Rize is the better pick

Rize has team plans, client billing reports and integrations built for time sheets. It runs on any laptop, because its AI runs in the cloud. OGAD needs a computer that can run a small local model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can.

If you bill clients by the hour and need team reports, Rize is built for that. If you want to see your own time privately, and on Linux, use OGAD.

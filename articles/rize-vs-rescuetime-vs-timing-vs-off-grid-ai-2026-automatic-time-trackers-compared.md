You bill clients and need team plans and billing reports, and you're fine with Gemini, Anthropic and OpenAI reading your window titles. It runs on any laptop, because its AI is in the cloud.

## Pick RescueTime if…

You want to change habits, not just see them. Focus Sessions block distractions, and Solo+ adds timesheets. It also has phone apps.

## Pick Timing if…

You are on a Mac and bill by the hour. Its reports, rounding and invoicing are strong, and you can leave its AI summaries off.

## Why Off Grid AI wins here

This page is about seeing your time without giving it away. Read the table's privacy rows.

- **Data and AI both local.** Off Grid AI keeps screen moments on your disk and writes summaries with a local model. Rize sends activity to three AI companies. Timing's AI runs on Google's Vertex AI. RescueTime stores your titles and URLs on its side.
- **Time by person.** Reflect shows who your time went to, from your calls, mail and chats. No rival lists this.
- **Every computer.** Off Grid AI is the only one with Linux. Timing is Mac only, and Rize's Linux is "coming soon".
- **More than a time sheet.** The same day of work feeds Memory, Meetings, God's morning brief and actions you approve.
- **One price.** $79.99 once, against $252 to $864 over three years.

Honest limits. Off Grid AI has no timesheets, billing or distraction blocking. It needs a computer that can run a small local model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can. On Windows and Linux you can run the analysis overnight, so your computer stays fast while you work.

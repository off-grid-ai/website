Notion AI Meeting Notes is handy if your team already lives in Notion. You type /meet, press Start, and the notes land in a page. No bot joins the call.

But Notion's [help page](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes) sets three limits. You need the Business or Enterprise plan. Desktop audio goes "directly to our sub-processors for real-time transcription". And it is "not currently supported while offline".

Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) Pro records without a bot too. **It transcribes and summarises on your own computer, works offline, and costs one payment**, not a seat on a higher plan.

## Where your call goes

Notion lists the sub-processors it uses to record, transcribe and summarise: OpenAI, Anthropic, Fireworks, Baseten, X.AI and AssemblyAI. It says they don't store the audio, and it keeps a retry copy for up to 3 days if processing fails.

That's a clear policy. It is still six outside companies hearing your call. With OGAD and local models selected, the audio, the transcript and the summary stay on your disk.

## You can still keep Notion

OGAD isn't a replacement for your Notion workspace. It connects to it. Notion is one of the tools Pro's actions work with: Pro drafts the update from the follow-ups, and it runs only when you say yes. Notion holds the shared notes. Your computer does the listening.

## How a call works in OGAD

1. Join a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call. OGAD sees it and records two tracks: the call's audio and your microphone.
2. For a meeting in a room, press **Record meeting**.
3. Press **Stop**. A local Whisper model writes the transcript, and a local text model writes the summary, decisions and follow-ups.

The steps are in [how to take AI meeting notes on a Mac without a bot](/articles/how-to-take-automatic-ai-meeting-notes-on-your-mac-in-2026-without-a-meeting-bot/).

## When Notion AI Meeting Notes is the better pick

If your company is already on Notion Business, the notes are included, they land straight in shared pages, and your team can see them right away. Notion also offers recording on its phone app through the microphone.

If you're on Notion's Free or Plus plan, take calls offline, or don't want outside AI companies hearing your meetings, use OGAD and send the result to Notion when you approve it.

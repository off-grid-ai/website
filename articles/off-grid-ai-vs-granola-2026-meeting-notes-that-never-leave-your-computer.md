## How a call becomes notes on your computer

1. Join a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call as usual. OGAD sees the call and starts recording. You can also press **Record meeting** yourself, for example for a meeting in a room.
2. It records two tracks: the call's audio and your microphone. So your voice and theirs stay apart in the transcript.
3. When you stop, a local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model writes the summary, the decisions and who owes what.
4. Open **Meetings** to read it, check a line against the recording, or ask a question about it.

The call itself still uses Zoom's or Teams's network, as it always does. The note-taking is the part that stays on your machine.

## When Granola is the better pick

Be fair to yourself. Granola has shared Team Spaces for notes across a company. It also runs well on any laptop, because the heavy work happens on its servers. If your company already pays for Granola and is happy with its cloud, keep it. If your calls carry client, legal, medical or deal details you'd rather not send to three AI companies, run the notes on your own machine.

## How Vault works

1. Open **Vault** in OGAD and set a master password.
2. Save the 24-word recovery phrase somewhere safe. With the vault file and its recovery data, it lets you back in if you forget the password.
3. Add logins, payment cards, API keys, secure notes and files. Everything is encrypted on your disk.
4. Lock it when you finish. Only you can unlock it.
5. To fill logins in Chrome, [request the browser extension](/articles/how-to-fill-website-logins-from-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) and pair it with the desktop app.

Keep [sensitive files](/articles/how-to-store-sensitive-files-in-an-encrypted-local-vault-in-2026/) and [API keys](/articles/how-to-store-api-keys-securely-on-your-computer-in-2026-no-cloud-vault/) in the same Vault. Run the [Security check](/articles/how-to-find-weak-and-reused-passwords-in-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) to find weak and reused passwords.

## Your vault works with your AI agent

Off Grid Vault fills logins in your own browser, matched to the website's domain. Off Grid's [Web Use](/articles/how-to-automate-tasks-in-your-signed-in-browser-with-local-ai-in-2026/) runs agent tasks in that same signed-in browser. So an agent task can sign in without you pasting a password into a chat, and the password never goes to a model. Web Use and Computer Use are Pro features.

## When Keeper is the better pick

For families and phone users, Keeper is the better password manager. Family gives 5 people private vaults, unlimited shared folders and emergency access. Its [download page](https://www.keepersecurity.com/download.html) lists apps for macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android, a web vault and six browser extensions. It stores and fills [passkeys](https://www.keepersecurity.com/personal.html) on any device. Off Grid AI Vault does not fill passwords on your phone and has no family plan.

If you share passwords with family or need them on your phone, keep Keeper. If you want your passwords, keys and files in a vault that no company holds, use Off Grid AI Vault.

## FAQ

**How much does Keeper cost?**
On 30 October 2026, Keeper lists Personal at $42.99 a year and Family at $91.99 a year for 5 users. BreachWatch dark web alerts are an add-on from $26.99 a year.

**Is there a free Keeper plan?**
Yes. Keeper Free is limited to one mobile device and 10 records for passwords and passkeys, with no sharing. Paid plans add unlimited devices and sync.

**Where does Keeper store my vault?**
Keeper says it stores each user's vault as encrypted ciphertext in its cloud vault, hosted by AWS. The data is encrypted on your device first.

**Do I need an account for Off Grid AI Vault?**
No. Vault lives in Off Grid AI Desktop on your computer. You unlock it with a master password, and you can recover it with a 24-word recovery phrase.

**Can Off Grid AI fill passwords in my browser?**
Yes, in Chrome, through the Off Grid AI browser extension, which is available by request. The desktop app must be running, paired and unlocked. Keeper's autofill works in more browsers and on phones.

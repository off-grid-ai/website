## How Reflect works

1. Turn on capture in **Replay** with **Resume capture**. OGAD saves moments from your screen as you work, on your disk.
2. A local model reads each moment and notes the app, the project and the people involved.
3. Open **Reflect**. You see your day by project, app and person, plus your focus time.
4. Switch to the week view to see where your time goes over several days.

You can exclude apps from capture, pause it, and delete any moment. On Windows and Linux you can run the analysis overnight, so your computer stays fast while you work.

## When RescueTime is the better pick

RescueTime helps you change habits, not just see them. It has Focus Sessions that block distractions, goals and alerts, and a daily target. Solo+ adds timesheets with clients, projects, billable rates and exports, and there are team plans. It also has iOS and Android apps. Off Grid AI has none of those blocking or billing tools.

If you want a tool that pushes you to focus, or timesheets for clients, RescueTime is built for that. If you want to see your own time privately, by project and person, use OGAD.

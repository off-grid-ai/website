## Same local idea, different job

Both apps run Whisper on your own machine. Superwhisper's FAQ adds that offline models "only run really well on Apple Silicon macs", and Intel Macs work best with its cloud models. OGAD has the same honest limit: local speed depends on your computer and the model you pick. The app shows which models fit before you download.

Where they part is what happens after the words land.

- **Superwhisper** puts text at your cursor, with AI modes to reformat it.
- **OGAD** puts text at your cursor too. Then the same app records your calls, keeps your screens and documents in a local memory, and lists who owes what.

## How dictation works in OGAD

1. Open **Voice**, then **Voice settings**. Pick **Whisper** and download a model.
2. Turn on **Paste at cursor**. Choose hold-to-talk or toggle, and set your shortcut.
3. Click into any text field and hold the shortcut while you speak.

The steps are in [how to dictate into any Mac app](/articles/how-to-dictate-into-any-mac-app-with-local-ai-in-2026-no-cloud-transcription/), with guides for [Windows](/articles/how-to-dictate-into-windows-apps-with-local-ai-in-2026/) and [Linux](/articles/how-to-dictate-into-linux-apps-with-local-ai-in-2026/).

## When Superwhisper is the better pick

Superwhisper has a free plan with unlimited local Whisper dictation. It has dictation apps for iOS and Android, and its FAQ says a Pro license works on as many of your devices as you like. If you want dictation only, on your phone as well as your Mac, Superwhisper is the simpler choice.

If you work on Linux, record meetings on Windows, or want your dictated notes and calls to feed one memory and a follow-up list, use OGAD.

Comet is Perplexity's browser with an assistant built in. Its [page](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet) calls it "the AI browser that acts as a personal assistant". To use it, you move your browsing into Comet. And your prompts go to Perplexity. Comet's [privacy notice](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/legal/comet-privacy-notice) says it collects "what you type as a prompt".

Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) takes the other road. You keep Chrome. The Off Grid AI extension puts the assistant beside your page, and **the model runs on your own computer**. With a local model selected, the page and your question stay on your machine.

## How a browser task runs on your computer

1. Install the [Off Grid AI extension](/browser-extension/) in Chrome and keep OGAD running.
2. In the extension, open **Settings > Desktop** and choose **Pair with desktop**. Check that the six words match, then approve in OGAD.
3. Ask about the page you have open. Plain chat gets read-only tools, so it reads and answers.
4. Turn on **Agent** when you want it to click and move through pages. It uses your signed-in session, so you do not log in again somewhere new.
5. When a task wants to send, create or delete something, OGAD shows the action. You approve it or reject it.

The extension refuses one-time codes, card numbers and CAPTCHAs in code. Sign-ins come from your Vault or from you. The websites still use the internet as normal. The thinking happens on your machine.

## When Comet is the better pick

Comet is a full browser with Perplexity's search built in. If you want answers from the open web with citations, that is Perplexity's strength. Comet also has its own phone apps on iOS and Android. And it runs on any laptop, because the heavy work happens on Perplexity's servers. OGAD needs a computer that can run a local model. Most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which models fit.

Off Grid AI's extension is on Chrome today. If you live in Brave, Edge, Arc or Firefox, join the waitlist. If your work pages hold client, legal or deal details, keep the model on your own machine.

## FAQ

**Is there a Perplexity Comet alternative that runs locally?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Desktop runs the model on your Mac, Windows or Linux computer. Its extension brings that model into Chrome, beside the page you have open. With a local model selected, your prompt and the page go to your own computer, not to a cloud AI.

**Does Comet send my prompts to Perplexity?**
Yes. Comet's privacy notice says Perplexity collects what you type as a prompt and which links you click. It also says Perplexity uses data to improve its services, including its AI models.

**Do I have to switch browsers to use Off Grid AI?**
No. The Off Grid AI extension is on the Chrome Web Store today. Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox have a waitlist. Your logins, tabs and bookmarks stay where they are.

**Is Off Grid AI free?**
Chat in the browser is free. Browser tasks, desktop tools and the Vault need Pro, which costs $79.99 once for lifetime access on 30 October 2026, a limited-time price.

**Does Off Grid AI act without asking?**
Not for sends, creates and deletes. Those wait for your approval in Off Grid AI Desktop. Plain browser chat only gets read-only tools.

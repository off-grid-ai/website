## No bot in the room

Otter's best-known feature is OtterPilot, a bot that joins your Zoom, Meet or Teams call. Some clients don't like seeing it, and some companies block it.

OGAD never joins the call. It sees that a Zoom, Teams or Google Meet call has started and records two tracks: the call's audio and your microphone. For a meeting in a room, press **Record meeting** and it uses the microphone. Either way, nothing new shows up in the participant list.

## What you get after the call

When you stop, a local Whisper model writes the transcript. A local text model then writes:

- a summary of the call,
- the decisions made,
- the follow-ups, with who owes what,
- the screens that were shared.

All of it is searchable later, next to everything else Off Grid AI remembers about that client or project.

## When Otter is the better pick

Otter has a web app and shared team workspaces, and it runs on any laptop because its servers do the work. OGAD needs a computer that can run a speech model; most Apple silicon Macs and recent PCs can, and the app shows which models fit before you download.

If you want transcripts in a browser tab with nothing installed, Otter is simpler. If you want them private, unlimited and paid for once, use OGAD.

## Local, and in the browser you already use

Switching browsers has a real cost. Your logins, saved sessions, extensions and habits live in Chrome. OGAD leaves them there. The extension pairs with the desktop app, and the model runs in OGAD.

1. Install the [Off Grid AI extension](/browser-extension/) in Chrome and keep OGAD running.
2. In the extension, open **Settings > Desktop** and choose **Pair with desktop**. Check that the six words match, then approve in OGAD.
3. Ask about the page you have open. Plain chat gets read-only tools, so it reads and answers.
4. Turn on **Agent** for a task that needs clicks across pages. It uses the sessions you already have.
5. When the task wants to send, create or delete something, OGAD shows the action. You approve it or reject it.

You also choose the model. OGAD shows which models fit your computer, and it can use compatible GGUF files you already have. See [how to summarise web pages with local AI in your browser](/articles/how-to-summarise-web-pages-with-local-ai-in-your-browser-in-2026/).

## When Genspark is the better pick

Genspark ships a whole browser with an ad blocker, a large MCP Store and a shopping helper that compares deals and reviews. If you want a new browser and free on-device chat out of the box, it is a reasonable pick. It also runs on Intel Macs. OGAD's Mac app needs Apple silicon.

Check one thing first. Genspark's page does not say whether Super Agent and Autopilot run on your device too. If your pages hold client, legal or deal details, ask before you hand them to an agent. With OGAD, you pick a local model and the whole task stays on your machine. Its extension is on Chrome today; Edge, Brave, Arc and Firefox have a waitlist.

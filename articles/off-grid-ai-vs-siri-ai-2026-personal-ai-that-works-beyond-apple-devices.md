## Locked to one brand

Siri AI runs on iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27 and visionOS 27. Apple names no Windows or Android version. If you use a Windows laptop at work, or your partner has an Android phone, Siri AI is not there.

Region matters too. Apple says Siri AI will not be available at first in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS, and is not available in China for now.

Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) runs on Mac, Windows and Linux. Off Grid AI Mobile (OGAM) runs on iPhone and Android. One Pro key covers up to 5 devices, of any brand.

## Partly cloud, partly Google

Apple is careful with privacy. It says that when Private Cloud Compute handles a request, personal data is "not stored nor made accessible to Apple". But the request still leaves your device. Apple also says its new models were built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models. You don't choose the model.

Off Grid AI runs the model you download, on your own hardware. Turn off Wi-Fi and it still answers. You can swap models when a better one comes out, and the app shows which ones fit your device before you download.

## Memory you can use today

Siri AI draws on your messages, email and photos. Apple's conversation features, Siri Recap and Live Rewind, arrive "in beta later this year", and Live Rewind needs an Apple Watch Series 12 or Ultra 4.

Off Grid AI Pro remembers your work now, on your computer:

- **Memory.** Screens, meetings, mail and documents become one local memory you can search and ask.
- **Meetings.** OGAD detects Zoom, Teams and Google Meet calls and records the call audio and your mic as two tracks, with no bot. A local Whisper model writes the full transcript, and a local text model writes the summary, decisions and follow-ups.
- **God, your chief of staff.** A morning brief, meeting prep with last time's notes, and what people owe you.
- **Actions you approve.** Replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion. Nothing runs without your yes.
- **Sync.** Your phone and computer stay in sync, device to device. No server keeps a copy.

## When Siri AI is the better pick

If you live in Apple's world, Siri AI is built in and needs no setup. It works with your Apple apps, acts across the system, and reads what is on screen. It reaches your Apple Watch and Vision Pro. Off Grid AI is an app you install, and it cannot control the operating system the way Apple's own assistant can.

Use both if you like. Let Siri set your timers. Use Off Grid AI for the work you want private, portable and on any device.

## FAQ

**Does Siri AI run entirely on my device?**
No. Apple's announcement says Siri AI's models run on the device and on Private Cloud Compute servers. Apple says personal data sent to Private Cloud Compute is not stored or made accessible to Apple. Off Grid AI can run every request on your own device with local models.

**Which devices does Siri AI need?**
Apple says Siri AI needs an Apple Intelligence device, such as iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or later, or an M1 or later Mac or iPad, on iOS 27, macOS 27 or the matching software. Apple lists no Windows, Linux or Android version.

**Is Siri AI free?**
Siri AI comes with supported Apple devices. Apple says its server-side features have daily usage limits, and that expanded access will be available for a fee in the future.

**Can Siri AI take meeting notes?**
Apple says Siri Recap, which gives a title and key points for a conversation, arrives in beta later this year. Off Grid AI Desktop Pro records Zoom, Teams and Google Meet calls today and writes a full transcript, summary and follow-ups on your computer.

**Does Off Grid AI work on iPhone?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Mobile runs local models for chat, voice, vision and images on iPhone and Android. With Pro, it syncs with your computer device to device, with no server keeping a copy.

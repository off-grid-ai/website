## What LastPass said about its 2022 security incident

LastPass described the incident on its own [notice of security incident](https://blog.lastpass.com/posts/notice-of-recent-security-incident) page and in a [March 2023 update](https://blog.lastpass.com/posts/security-incident-update-recommended-actions). In its own words and dates:

- **25 August 2022.** LastPass said an unauthorized party took portions of source code and technical information from its development environment.
- **15 September 2022.** LastPass said it found no evidence of access to customer data or encrypted vaults in that incident.
- **30 November 2022.** LastPass said it found activity in a third-party cloud storage service it shares with its affiliate GoTo.
- **22 December 2022.** LastPass said the attacker copied "a backup of customer vault data". Website usernames and passwords were encrypted. Some data, such as website URLs, was not. LastPass said the master password "is never known to LastPass".
- **1 March 2023.** LastPass said the attacker targeted a senior DevOps engineer through vulnerable third-party software. It said it had seen no attacker activity since 26 October 2022.

The point for this comparison is where copies live. A cloud vault has a copy on the vendor's systems and in its backups. Off Grid AI Vault has no copy on our servers. That moves the job to you: keep a strong master password, keep your recovery phrase safe and back up the vault file yourself.

## How Vault works

1. Open **Vault** in OGAD and set a master password.
2. Save the 24-word recovery phrase somewhere safe. With the vault file and its recovery data, it lets you back in if you forget the password.
3. Add logins, payment cards, API keys, secure notes and files. Everything is encrypted on your disk.
4. Lock it when you finish. Only you can unlock it.
5. To fill logins in Chrome, [request the browser extension](/articles/how-to-fill-website-logins-from-your-off-grid-ai-vault-in-2026/) and pair it with the desktop app.

Moving from LastPass? The browser Vault has an **Import from LastPass** option for a LastPass CSV export. [Import a small sample first](/articles/how-to-import-saved-passwords-and-secure-notes-into-off-grid-ai-in-2026/), check the records, then delete the CSV.

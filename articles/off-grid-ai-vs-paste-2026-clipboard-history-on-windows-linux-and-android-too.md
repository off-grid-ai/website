## How Clipboard works

1. Turn on Clipboard in OGAD. New copies are added while the app runs.
2. Each item shows the app and device it came from. A PDF is previewed as text.
3. Search by word, or open **quick open** over any app and type, such as "Sam".
4. Pair your phone with Pro Sync and choose to share copied text. A note copied on your iPhone shows up on your computer. On Android, pick **Copy to Off Grid AI** from the text selection menu; see [how to copy text on Android and paste it on your Mac](/articles/how-to-copy-text-on-android-and-paste-it-on-your-mac-in-2026-without-internet/).

You choose what syncs, and you can keep private items out. See [how to exclude private files and clipboard items from sync](/articles/how-to-exclude-private-files-and-clipboard-items-from-device-sync-in-off-grid-ai-in-2026/).

## FAQ

**Is there a Paste alternative for Windows and Linux?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Desktop Pro keeps a searchable clipboard history on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Paste lists Mac, iPhone and iPad.

**How much does Paste cost?**
Paste Personal is $2.49 a month, or $29.99 a year. Paste's pricing page also mentions a lifetime option but does not list its price.

**Where does Paste keep my clipboard?**
Paste's privacy policy says your history is stored on your device. With iCloud Sync on, a copy goes to your personal iCloud, not to Paste's servers.

**How does Off Grid AI sync my clipboard?**
With Pro Sync, copied text can be shared between your own paired devices, encrypted, device to device. No server keeps a copy, and you choose what syncs.

**What can I copy into Off Grid AI Clipboard?**
Text, links, images and files. Each item shows the app and device it came from, and you can search all of it.

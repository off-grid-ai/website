LUCI, from Memories.ai, is a strong local memory app. It records your screen and meetings, keeps them **encrypted on your own disk**, blacks out cards, keys and passwords, and runs local models. It is free. If you want local memory, LUCI is a fair choice.

So what is the difference? LUCI is built to hand your memory to other agents, such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex. Those agents often run in the cloud. **Off Grid AI Desktop (OGAD) Pro keeps the memory local and does the follow-up work itself, with a local model and your approval.**

## Four questions to ask any memory app

1. **Capture.** What does it save, and when?
2. **Storage.** Where does the history live?
3. **Inference.** Which model reads it, and where does that model run?
4. **Actions.** Can it do work for you, and who approves it?

LUCI and OGAD give the same answer to the first three. The fourth is where they part.

## Where the two really differ

**Where the memory goes next.** LUCI is careful about storage. Its [Claude page](https://luci.memories.ai/integrations/claude) says "The memory itself stays on your machine." It is also honest about the next step: what an agent pulls joins that conversation, and "If the agent runs in someone else's cloud, that conversation goes there." So your privacy depends on which agent you connect.

OGAD does not need an outside agent to act. It drafts the reply, the issue or the prep note with a local model, and it waits for your yes.

**More than recall.** OGAD builds a **Day** view with a journal, a timeline and to-dos, and **People** pages for each person you work with. **God** gives you a morning brief, meeting prep and what people owe you. **Reflect** shows where your time went.

**Linux and phones.** OGAD runs on Linux, and OGAM runs on iPhone and Android, synced with your computer device to device. LUCI's docs list Mac and Windows.

## How OGAD remembers your day

1. Open **Replay** and choose **Resume capture**. The status shows **Capturing**. Choose **Pause capture** at any time.
2. OGAD saves sampled moments from your screen to your disk.
3. A local vision model reads each moment and writes a short note: the app, the project and the people.
4. On Windows and Linux you can choose **Overnight**, so the analysis runs from 03:00 to 08:00 and your computer stays fast by day.

You can exclude apps and delete any moment. See [how to delete saved screen activity](/articles/how-to-delete-saved-screen-activity-and-ai-work-notes-in-off-grid-ai-on-mac-in-2026/).

## FAQ

**Is LUCI local?**
Yes. LUCI's docs say recordings, notes and memory stay on your computer, and it keeps working with the network off. Its home page lists Phi, Qwen, Llama and DeepSeek as local models.

**Is LUCI free?**
Yes. LUCI's pricing page says it is free forever, with no Pro tier and no paid upgrade.

**What happens when LUCI gives my memory to Claude or Codex?**
LUCI says what an agent pulls becomes part of that conversation. If the agent runs in someone else's cloud, that conversation goes there. Off Grid AI answers and drafts with local models on your computer.

**Does LUCI run on Linux?**

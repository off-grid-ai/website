## What changed on 6 October

Anthropic's [help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15520349-use-claude-cowork-on-web-desktop-and-mobile) lists the change:

- New Cowork tasks on Pro and Max run in the cloud. There is no setting to choose where a task runs.
- The "Only on your computer" option was removed.
- Tasks and scheduled tasks you started on your computer before the change keep running there.
- Your folders stay on your computer. Claude reaches them through the desktop app, only while it is open.

Anthropic's [safety page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-use-claude-cowork-safely) says Claude's work runs on its servers in an isolated environment. Deleted tasks leave backend storage within 30 days.

## How a task works in Off Grid AI

1. Ask in chat: "Draft a reply to Sam with the dates we agreed at kickoff."
2. A local model reads your memory: the meeting, the thread, your notes. It writes the draft and shows its sources.
3. The send goes to **Actions** as a pending approval. Nothing goes out.
4. You approve or reject it. The approval is written to a log.

For pages on the web, **Web Use** works through a browser inside OGAD. **Computer Use** works with the apps on your screen. Live sites still need the internet. ## When Claude Cowork is the better pick

Be fair to yourself. Cowork makes long, finished work: decks, reports, spreadsheets and designs. A frontier Claude model is stronger at that than a model that fits on a laptop.

Cloud tasks also keep running when your laptop is closed, and you can steer them from web and mobile. OGAD's tasks run on your computer, so it must be awake and the app open. OGAD also needs a machine that fits a good local model. The app shows which models fit before you download.

If you write polished files every day and your company is fine with Anthropic's cloud, Cowork is a strong tool. If your work holds client, legal or deal details you want on your own disk, with a yes before each action, run OGAD. Many people will use both.

## FAQ

**Does Claude Cowork run on my computer or in the cloud?**
In the cloud, for new tasks on Pro and Max. Anthropic's help page says that from 6 October 2026, new Cowork tasks on those plans run in the cloud and the "Only on your computer" option was removed. Tasks started on your computer before that stay there.

**How much does Claude Cowork cost?**
Cowork is part of Claude's paid plans. Anthropic lists Pro at $20 a month, or $200 a year, and Max from $100 a month. Off Grid AI Pro is $79.99 once on 30 October 2026, a limited-time lifetime price.

**Does Claude Cowork ask before it acts?**
It depends on the mode. Anthropic's safety page says Auto mode is the default on Pro, Max and Team, and Claude decides whether to ask before write or delete connector tools. Manual mode asks before actions.

**Is there a Cowork alternative that keeps my files local?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Desktop Pro keeps its memory on your disk. With local models selected, the model also runs on your computer. Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion still need the internet when you use them.

**Can Off Grid AI write documents and slides like Cowork?**
Off Grid AI drafts replies, issues and docs through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion, and answers from your own memory. For long, polished files such as slide decks, Cowork with a frontier model is stronger.

## What God does each morning

God is your chief of staff in OGAD Pro. It works from what you already did, on your computer:

1. **A morning brief.** Your meetings, your to-dos and what is waiting for your approval, as a local notification.
2. **Meeting prep.** Notes from last time and the sources behind them, before the call starts.
3. **What people owe you.** To-dos from calls and mail, with owners and where they came from.
4. **Routines.** Scheduled tasks such as the weekday brief, meeting prep and an approvals digest.
5. **Rules.** Lines it never crosses, such as "never send anything to Acme without asking."

Replies and issues go out through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion only after your yes.

## The rest of Pro

- **Memory.** Screens, meetings, mail and documents become one local memory you can search and ask.
- **Reflect.** Where your day went, by project, app and person. No timers.
- **Vault, Clipboard and Sync.** Encrypted secrets, a searchable clipboard, and your phone and computer in sync, device to device.

One key covers up to 5 devices.

## When Motion is the better pick

Motion's core is auto-scheduling. It puts your tasks on your calendar and re-plans when things change. Off Grid AI does not do that. Motion also has AI projects, tasks, docs and a wiki, with desktop and mobile apps.

If you want software to arrange your week for you, keep Motion. If you mostly pay for its AI chat and notetaker, Off Grid AI does those jobs on your own computer for a one-time price.

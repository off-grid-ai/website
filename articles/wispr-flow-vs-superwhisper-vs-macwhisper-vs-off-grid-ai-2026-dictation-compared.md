## Pick Wispr Flow if…

You dictate mostly on your phone, and you are happy for every sentence to go to a cloud first. Its cloud models clean up filler words fast on any laptop.

## Pick Superwhisper if…

You want dictation only, on a Mac or Windows PC and your phone. Its free plan has unlimited local Whisper. Note its FAQ says offline models run best on Apple silicon Macs.

## Pick MacWhisper if…

You work only on a Mac and want a focused transcript tool at €64 once. It also records calls without a bot.

## Pick Off Grid AI if…

You work on Linux, or across Mac and Windows. You want your words to stay on your computer and feed one memory of your calls and work.

## FAQ

**Which dictation app works offline?**
Superwhisper, MacWhisper and Off Grid AI all transcribe on your computer, so they work offline after the model downloads. Wispr Flow's privacy page says transcription always happens in the cloud.

**Which dictation app works on Linux?**
Off Grid AI. Its dictation into other apps works on Mac (Apple silicon), Windows and Linux. Wispr Flow and Superwhisper list Mac and Windows. MacWhisper is a Mac app.

**Which is cheapest, Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, MacWhisper or Off Grid AI?**
For dictation only, Superwhisper's free plan and MacWhisper's free version cost nothing, and MacWhisper Pro is €64 once. Off Grid AI Pro is $79.99 once on 30 October 2026, a limited-time price, and adds meetings, memory and actions. Wispr Flow Pro is $12 a month billed yearly.

**Can I dictate on my phone with Off Grid AI?**
Not into other apps. Off Grid AI's dictation into other apps is for computers. The phone app has voice input inside its own chat. Wispr Flow and Superwhisper have iOS and Android dictation.

**Do these dictation apps record meetings?**
Off Grid AI records meetings without a bot on Mac, Windows and Linux. MacWhisper records calls without a bot on the Mac. Superwhisper lists Meetings as Mac only. Wispr Flow's pages that we checked do not list meeting recording.

## Four questions to ask any memory app

1. **Capture.** What does it save, and when?
2. **Storage.** Where does the history live?
3. **Inference.** Which model reads it, and where does that model run?
4. **Actions.** Can it do work for you, and who approves it?

Recall and OGAD both keep capture, storage and inference on your computer. The difference is where they run and what they do after they remember.

## Where the two really differ

You also need BitLocker or Device Encryption, Windows Hello Enhanced Sign-in Security with a biometric option, and at least 50 GB free to turn it on. OGAD needs no NPU. Any PC that runs a small local vision model can use it.

**Speed during the day.** On Windows and Linux, OGAD has an **Overnight** mode. It saves screenshots as you work and runs the heavy analysis from 03:00 to 08:00. Your PC stays fast by day, and your **Day** view is ready by morning. OGAD runs on [Mac](/articles/a-private-microsoft-recall-alternative-on-your-mac-in-2026-local-ai-reviews-your-day-overnight/) and [Linux](/articles/a-private-microsoft-recall-alternative-on-linux-in-2026-local-ai-reviews-your-day-overnight/) too, and one Pro key covers up to 5 devices.

## How OGAD remembers your day

1. Open **Replay** and choose **Resume capture**. The status shows **Capturing**. Choose **Pause capture** at any time.
2. OGAD saves sampled moments from your screen to your disk.
3. A local vision model reads each moment and writes a short note: the app, the project and the people.
4. Open **Day** for a journal, a timeline and your to-dos. Ask a question and get the answer with its source.

You can exclude apps and delete any moment. See [how to delete saved screen activity](/articles/how-to-delete-saved-screen-activity-and-ai-work-notes-in-off-grid-ai-on-mac-in-2026/).

## When Microsoft Recall is the better pick

If you already own a Copilot+ PC, Recall costs nothing extra and needs no model download. Its security is built into Windows: encrypted snapshots, Windows Hello for every launch, and the Pluton security processor. If you have a Copilot+ PC and only want to search your past screens, try Recall first. If you want screen memory on any PC, Mac or Linux computer, with meetings and approved actions, use OGAD.

## FAQ

**Does Microsoft Recall send my snapshots to Microsoft?**
Microsoft's support page says Recall saves snapshots encrypted on your local drive and does not share them with Microsoft or third parties. Screenshots you attach to feedback are sent.

**Which PCs can run Microsoft Recall?**
A Copilot+ PC that meets the Secured-core standard, with a 40 TOPS NPU, 16 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage. You also need BitLocker or Device Encryption and Windows Hello Enhanced Sign-in Security.

**Is there a Microsoft Recall alternative for older PCs, Mac or Linux?**
Yes. Off Grid AI Desktop Pro saves sampled screen moments on your disk and reads them with a local model. It runs on Windows PCs without an NPU, on Mac (Apple silicon) and on Linux.

**Is Microsoft Recall on by default?**
No. Saving snapshots is off by default. You turn it on in Settings or when you first open Recall. Off Grid AI capture is also off until you choose Resume capture.

**How much does Off Grid AI cost?**
On 30 October 2026 Off Grid AI Pro is $79.99 once for lifetime access, a limited-time price, or $7.99 a month. It covers up to 5 devices.

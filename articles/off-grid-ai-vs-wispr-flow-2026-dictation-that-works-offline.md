## How dictation works in OGAD

1. Open **Voice**, then **Voice settings**. Pick **Whisper** and download a model. For languages other than English, choose a multilingual one.
2. Turn on **Paste at cursor**. Choose hold-to-talk or toggle, and set your shortcut.
3. Click into any text field: email, Slack, your code editor, a document.
4. Hold the shortcut, speak, and let go. The text lands where your cursor was.

On a Mac, allow microphone and Accessibility access so the shortcut and paste work in every app. The steps are in [how to dictate into any Mac app](/articles/how-to-dictate-into-any-mac-app-with-local-ai-in-2026-no-cloud-transcription/), with guides for [Windows](/articles/how-to-dictate-into-windows-apps-with-local-ai-in-2026/) and [Linux](/articles/how-to-dictate-into-linux-apps-with-local-ai-in-2026/).

Dictation needs only the speech model. You don't need a large chat model for it.

## Why local dictation matters more than it sounds

You dictate the things you'd rather not type: a reply to a hard email, a note after a doctor's visit, a client's numbers, a draft you'll rewrite three times. That is a lot of private speech going to a server. With OGAD, the audio is turned into text on your computer and nothing is sent.

It also works on a plane, on a train, or behind a strict office firewall.

## What the free app already does

You may not need to pay at all for the daily chat. OGAD Free gives you:

- **Chat.** Ask, draft, summarise and write code with a local model.
- **Files with cited answers.** Add PDFs and documents, then ask about them. Answers point to the source.
- **Voice.** Speak your prompt and hear the reply.
- **Images.** Make images on your own computer.
- **A local API.** Point your own tools at the model on your machine.

It needs no account, and once a model is downloaded, chat works offline. Read more in our guide to [a local, offline alternative to ChatGPT](/articles/a-local-offline-alternative-to-chatgpt-for-your-desktop-in-2026/).

## What Pro adds: memory of your work

ChatGPT remembers what you tell it. Off Grid AI Pro remembers what you did:

- **Memory.** Your screens, meetings, mail and documents become one local memory. Ask "what did Sam say about the rollout?" and get the answer with its source.
- **Meetings without a bot.** Calls recorded, transcribed and summarised on your computer.
- **Actions you approve.** Replies and issues through Gmail, Linear, Jira and Notion. Nothing goes out without your yes.
- **God, your chief of staff.** A morning brief, your routines and meeting prep.
- **Reflect, Vault, Clipboard and Sync.** Time by project and person, encrypted secrets, a searchable clipboard, and your phone and computer in sync, device to device.

One key covers up to 5 devices, desktop and mobile.

## When ChatGPT Plus is the better pick

Be honest about the gap. ChatGPT Plus runs OpenAI's newest frontier models on large servers. For the hardest reasoning, deep research across the web, and the best image quality, it is ahead of any model a laptop can run. It also runs on any device, because the heavy work happens on OpenAI's side.

If you need frontier-level answers every day, keep Plus. If most of your use is drafting, summaries and questions about your own files, and you would rather keep that work private, run it on your own computer.

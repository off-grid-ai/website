Changing phones should not mean guessing whether you need to buy the same app again.

OGAM (Off Grid AI Mobile) lets you activate your existing Pro licence on a new phone and manage the devices using it. Start with the licence key from your purchase email. If you need to free a device seat, the Sync device list lets you remove the old device from the licensed group.

Licence activation and device management need a connection to the licensing service. Moving access is also separate from moving your chats, files, and downloaded models.

## Activate the phone before retiring the old one

Keep your purchase email available while you set up the new phone. Open OGAM's Pro screen and choose the option to enter your licence key. Paste the key into the activation form and submit it.

Wait for **Pro activated** and the confirmation that Pro is active on this device. A completed app download alone does not establish the licence state.

The activation screen describes support for up to five devices. The actual licence and its device roster determine available access. If activation cannot complete, read the error before buying again or removing devices at random.

The licence and device-management flow is present in [OGAM 0.0.111](https://github.com/off-grid-ai/OGAM/releases/tag/v0.0.111).

## Free the old phone's place when you need it

From an already licensed device, open **Settings → Sync**. You can also reach this view through **Licensed devices** in the Pro licence section.

Find the old phone in the device list. Check the name carefully, open its removal action, and read the confirmation. For a licensed device, the confirmation is **Evict device**. It removes that device from the licensed group, frees its seat, and ends its Sync trust.

The old phone does not have to appear as currently connected for its licensed seat to be listed. If it is offline, removal of its saved local licence is queued until it reconnects. Removing a seat is not an instant remote wipe of an offline phone.

After the change succeeds, return to the new phone's activation flow if it was waiting for a free seat. If device removal reports an error, keep the old state in mind and resolve the connection or service error before assuming the seat is free.

## Access and data are two different moves

Activating Pro gives the new phone access to the relevant features. It does not by itself copy your conversations, files, or model downloads.

If you want that content, set up the appropriate device sync and transfers while the old phone is still available. Verify the important material on the new phone before retiring the old one.

Eviction ends the old device's Sync trust. The devices must pair again before Sync can reconnect. For that reason, finish the data transfer you need before removing the old device from the group.

This is also different from cancelling a recurring subscription. Device removal changes the licensed roster. Subscription changes use the billing route shown for your licence.

## A short replacement-phone checklist

1. Install OGAM on the new phone and have the purchase email ready.
2. Enter the existing licence key in the Pro activation flow.
3. If a seat needs to be freed, use an already licensed device's Sync list to remove the intended old phone.
4. Confirm that the new phone shows Pro as active.
5. Pair and transfer any content you want separately, before retiring the old device.

If you cannot access the key or any already licensed device, use the support route associated with your purchase. An offline local model cannot validate an unavailable licence record for you.

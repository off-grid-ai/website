## What's in the current build

Local models for writing, images, and voice. Supervised tasks in your apps and browser.

- **Chat** - local text and vision models with a reasoning mode.
- **Images** - create and edit with Z-Image-Turbo and SDXL-Lightning on your GPU.
- **Voice** - local transcription with Whisper and spoken replies with Kokoro.
- **Your model library** - a catalog of local models with sizes, plus Hugging Face search.
- **Approved tasks** - supervised Computer Use and Web Use. Pause, stop, or take over.
- **Task continuity** - confirmed outcomes help longer tasks continue.

# getoffgridai.co

This is a marketing and sales site. Every page sells Off Grid AI.

## Copy

- Say what Off Grid AI does for the reader and why it is good. Lead with the benefit.
- No limitations sections, no "what this does not do", no caveats beyond what the reader needs to act: device, RAM, free or Pro, price.
- Comparisons show only where Off Grid AI wins.
- Positive never means invented. Every claim is true and checkable: real numbers, dated where they change, from a source you can name.
- No live counts on the page (owners, seats left).
- Brand voice: `../brand/brand_tone_voice.md`. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, "Off Grid AI" in full.

## LinkedIn post copy

120,000 people have downloaded Off Grid AI.

The next goal is to make OGAM and OGAD free forever.

Knowledge work already lives across screens. But its context remains fragmented. Apps on the same device do not understand one another. Different devices know even less. People search, copy, repeat and reconnect the context themselves.

The destination is on-device intelligence for every knowledge worker. The kind of support once reserved for people who could hire an exceptional chief of staff: planning the day, preparing meetings, protecting attention and delaying information until the context is right.

Personal context should remain personal. Raw conversations and raw content stay on the device.

Free still has to fund the work. The model: enterprise value funds consumer access.

Every enterprise should become an intelligent enterprise. It works more efficiently through higher speed and lower cost. It works more effectively through higher-quality decisions and outputs. It can do work that was previously impossible.

OGAC gives an organization a reliable, auditable and compliant way to implement AI in regulated work. It connects employees, devices, data sources, systems, processes and models without forcing the organization to give up control.

OGAC is AWS for AI. Underneath it, roughly 50 composable open-source services handle the data, model, routing, orchestration, policy, evaluation, observability and operational layers required to run enterprise AI.

There are two enterprise wedges.

The first is Lovable, Bolt or Replit for the enterprise. A person describes an app, workflow or agent in plain English. It inherits approved data, organizational rules, access, guardrails, evaluation, audit and human review from the enterprise.

This means the people closest to a problem can build the capability they need. OGAC supplies the enterprise context and controls that make it safe to run in production.

The second is a private context layer at every node. Traditional device management provides inventory, compliance and remote control. Off Grid makes the device intelligent.

OGAD already sees and remembers the desktop workday locally. OGAM is bringing voice conversations into the same on-device memory model. Each node helps the person in the moment, while the sync layer shares only approved derived knowledge with the organization. Raw screenshots, audio and conversations remain on the device.

Together, the enterprise builder and intelligent nodes create an organizational brain. The best knowledge and judgment reach every person at the moment of work.

The business case starts with one important workflow: process more claims with the same team, intervene before delinquency rolls forward, give relationship managers compliant next-best actions or stop unreliable AI before release.

Pricing is intentionally transparent:

- 1-50 users: $149.50 per month
- 51-500 users: $2.99 per user per month
- 501+ users: enterprise licensing

For design partners, discovery, setup and the first implementation are included. Billing begins only after the PoC works and its owner signs off.

Enterprise adoption is a network game. This community can open the first doors.

Bring a workflow that matters. Introduce the person who owns it. Build the first proof with Wednesday. Or challenge the thesis.

Help build the intelligence infrastructure companies run on. Keep OGAM and OGAD free forever.

# Build a solution for your daily work. Pay $0.

## Small business design partners

I'm looking for small businesses with fewer than 50 people that want to use Off Grid AI in their daily work. Ideally, I'll work directly with an owner or someone who can choose and test a solution.

As a design partner, you work directly with me. Show me how your business works. We identify a problem worth solving, and you test what we build. I help you set up and implement the solution at no cost.

## What you get

- **Free setup and implementation.** Your cost as a design partner is $0.
- **Free lifetime product access.** If we create a product based even loosely on your input, you get free lifetime access to that product.
- **Free lifetime Off Grid AI Pro, if your idea is a good fit for Off Grid AI.** This applies even if we do not build the full solution together.

## Who this is for

Your business has fewer than 50 people. You have a task or process that you want to improve, and you can test a solution in your daily work.

I'm looking for recurring problems that would normally justify spending $99-$999 per month to solve. As a design partner, your cost is $0.

## What I need from you

I need your time and feedback. Show me how you handle the work today. Try the solution in your business. Tell me what works and what needs to change.

I want to build around real work and learn from the people doing it.

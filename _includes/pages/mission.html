01THE SHIFT

Intelligence is next.Intelligence is next.

Navigation used to belong to experts. Then it became ambient.Navigation used to belong to experts. Then it became ambient.

You needed a map, a compass, training. Then it was built into the device in your pocket, free and always on. You stopped thinking of it as a tool.

Intelligence is next. Not an app you open. Not rent you pay. Not a server that answers when you remember to ask. Ambient intelligence. Always on. Always yours.

The question isn't whether it happens. It's who builds it, on whose terms, and whose data pays for it.

02THE PROBLEM

The way it's being built today is wrong.The way it's being built today is wrong.

Your intelligence on a server you don't own is one acquisition, one pricing change, one bad quarter from gone.Your intelligence on a server you don't own is one acquisition, one pricing change, one bad quarter from gone.

Today your health questions, money decisions and 2am ideas go to servers you don't control, under terms you didn't read.

Some products promised local-first. Then they went cloud, got acquired, shut down overnight. Their users lost years of personal context. That already happened.

The problem isn't the companies. The problem is the architecture.

03THE HARDWARE

The infrastructure is already in your hands.The infrastructure is already in your hands.

The hardware for personal AI is already in your hands. Our job is to build the software that makes it useful.The hardware for personal AI is already in your hands. Our job is to build the software that makes it useful.

Your phone and computer already run local AI models for chat, writing and documents. Pick a model that fits, download it, run it offline.

No new device to wait for. Nothing to buy.

04PRIVACY

Privacy is not a feature. It's an architecture decision.Privacy is not a feature. It's an architecture decision.

The only guarantee your chats stay yours is that they never leave your device.The only guarantee your chats stay yours is that they never leave your device.

"We anonymise before storing." "You can opt out in settings." Policies change when the company does. Not a toggle. Not a promise. Architecture.

Open source.

If you can't audit it, you can't trust it. Anyone can verify what the software does.

No account.

No telemetry unless you opt in.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports are off until you turn them on. The list of what we collect is public.

05WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

A private secretary, for everyone.A private secretary, for everyone.

For two hundred years, a private intelligence layer belonged to the wealthy and the senior.For two hundred years, a private intelligence layer belonged to the wealthy and the senior.

A secretary knew your priorities, handled correspondence, prepared every meeting and tracked commitments, so you could do the work only you can do.

A Personal AI OS changes that. One intelligence layer across your phone and laptop, over your own network, no server in between. It preps your meetings before you ask and surfaces only what can't wait. It acts with your approval.

On devices billions already carry. On models that cost nothing to run. With chats and files that never leave your hands.

Democratize intelligence. Personal. Private. Ambient. On the hardware people already own.

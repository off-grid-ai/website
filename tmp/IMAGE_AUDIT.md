# Image audit: getoffgridai.co (all images, 8 Oct 2026)

Scope: the home tour (`preview-ui/page.jsx`, `WALK` plus scenes), every React page in `preview-ui/pages/*.jsx`, and every body image in `articles/`, `guides/` and `writing/`. Articles were audited in the current working tree, including the uncommitted re-match of about 150 images. Each distinct file was opened at its `-1760` or `-640` variant, or at full size where no variant exists. Bar: `tmp/FEEDBACK_LEDGER.md` rules 5, 26 and 28, plus the founder's "very apt" requirement. The demo persona is Sam Okafor at Acme Corp.

Founder rule applied: iPhone screenshots are acceptable on Android pages and articles. None is flagged for that reason. Where an Android context frames the phone, use the Magic UI Android frame.

Verdicts: **OK** means apt and clean. **WEAK** means it is clean but only loosely shows the claim, or a better capture is possible. **WRONG** means it shows a different feature or contradicts the copy. **UNCLEAN** means it shows fixtures, placeholders, artefacts, failed or empty states, debug text or personal-data traces.

Capture IDs: `D#` is a desktop capture from the seeded Playwright Electron run, both themes, exported as full size and `-1760`. `P#` is an iPhone capture on the connected device, both themes, exported as 1290 px and `-640`. Full specs are in "New captures needed" below.

## Summary numbers

- Home tour: 19 chapters, 37 screen usages (pairs counted once). **OK 18, WEAK 12, WRONG 2, UNCLEAN 5.**
- Product pages: about 75 usages across /desktop/, /mobile/, /pro/, /quick-start/, /download/, /thank-you/, /vision/ and /ogap/. **Mostly WEAK or UNCLEAN, from the same root files as the home tour.** /mobile/ "Larger models" is WRONG. Details in section 2.
- Articles: 443 body images in 379 files. **OK 76, WEAK 313, WRONG 16, UNCLEAN 38.**
  - 229 of these are `/assets/cover.png`, generic brand artwork, plus 5 of `cover-democratizing-intelligence.png`, the same artwork. Every one is WEAK because it illustrates nothing. 227 articles have only this image.
- Guides (`guides/*.md`) and writing (`writing/*.md`) have **no body images**. Nothing to fix, though guides like `ios-setup`, `remote-servers` and `knowledge-base` would benefit from P-series shots.
- Biggest single defects:
  1. `models-text` and its duplicate `models` show the e2e test model "SmolLM2-135M-Instruct-GGUF" on device. This affects 26 article usages, the home Models chapter, the desktop page, quick-start and the desktop Models carousel.
  2. `chat-ios-1` and `voice-ios-1` end the drafted reply with the placeholder "[Your Name]". This affects the home phone and voice chapters, the mobile hero, mobile Chat and Voice mode, and 7 articles.
  3. `replay` is Paused, records our own website, and has the wrong thread label. It is used on the desktop "It sees" section, the Pro Memory tab, the quick-start Pro step, the home Capture chapter and 2 articles. Ledger item 28 said Replay had been pulled, but it is back in all of these places.
  4. `artifacts` prints "Off Grid AI Pro, annual licence $49 / seat", which uses licence wording and contradicts live pricing. It is on the desktop Artifacts section and in 5 articles.
  5. `other-1` (SOS status bar, empty gallery, Voice 0) and `imagegen-2` (Android junk prompt) are in 6 and 4 article phone grids.
  6. `remote-ios-1` is used for Ollama and LM Studio articles even though Ollama and LM Studio are switched OFF in it. 5 articles.
